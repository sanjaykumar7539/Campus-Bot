Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
B.Sc. / M.Sc. Computer Science — PEOs
##
Objective
PEO1
Build a strong foundation in core Computer Science (algorithms, programming, data structures, databases, networks, OS) and applied domains.
PEO2
Develop industry-ready competencies through projects, internships, and modern toolchains; excel in software/system development roles.
PEO3
Demonstrate professional ethics, teamwork, communication, and leadership aligned with institutional values and societal needs.
PEO4
Pursue lifelong learning via higher studies, certifications, and research in emerging areas (AI, IoT, Cloud, Cyber Security, Data Science).
PEO5
Foster innovation and entrepreneurship by identifying real-world problems and delivering scalable technology solutions.
