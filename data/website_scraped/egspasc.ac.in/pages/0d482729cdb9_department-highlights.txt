Highlights — Department of Nutrition & Dietetics
Program Orientation:
B.Sc. Nutrition & Dietetics and M.Sc. Food Science & Nutrition focused on excellence in teaching, research, and service.
Faculty Expertise:
Active mentoring, research guidance, and wide domain knowledge.
Public Health Education:
Awareness on balanced diets, healthy eating habits, and lifestyle disease prevention.
School Collaborations:
Nutrition education programs and support for school food initiatives.
Community Outreach:
Camps and programs for underserved groups; workplace nutrition and wellness.
Practice-Ready Training:
Labs, hospital/community postings, ICDS exposure, diet counseling, and food quality control.
Speak with our advisors to align program pathways with careers in clinical dietetics, community nutrition, and the food industry.
