Department of Microbiology
Welcome to the Department of Microbiology. Our department is dedicated to providing high-quality education and research opportunities in the fascinating world of microbes. We aim to prepare our students for varied careers in medicine, industry, research, and beyond.
With a focus on both theoretical foundations and practical laboratory skills, we ensure our graduates are well-equipped to meet the challenges of the modern scientific landscape.
