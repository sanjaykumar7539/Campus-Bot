Department of Commerce
It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Our department is committed to delivering high-quality education, fostering innovative research, and preparing students to meet the challenges of an ever-evolving technological world.
We emphasize experiential and student-centric learning approaches such as Project-Based Learning (PBL), flipped classroom methodologies, and Design Thinking and Innovation (DTI) practices, enabling students to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world problems while nurturing creativity, critical thinking, and entrepreneurial skills.
