Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
##
PSO1
Apply computing and mathematical fundamentals to identify, analyze, and model real-world problems,
and architect effective software solutions.
(Aligned to PEO1–PEO3)
PSO2
Design, develop, deploy, and maintain applications (web / mobile / data-centric) using modern
languages, frameworks, databases, cloud and DevOps practices for Government and Private sectors.
(Aligned to PEO1–PEO3, PEO6)
PSO3
Demonstrate professional competence—effective communication, collaboration, leadership, and an
entrepreneurial mindset—while pursuing higher studies or career advancement through continuous learning.
(Aligned to PEO4–PEO6, PEO9)
PSO4
Practice ethical, legal, security, and societal responsibilities; build inclusive, user-centric solutions
that contribute positively to community and sustainability goals.
(Aligned to PEO7–PEO8)
