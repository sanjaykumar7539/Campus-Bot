Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
Biochemistry — PSOs
The programme equips students with strong foundations in biomolecular structure–function,
metabolic pathways, and real-world applications across health, research, and industry.
##
Programme Specific Outcome
PSO1
Demonstrate comprehensive knowledge of biochemical principles, biomolecules, metabolism, and the molecular basis of life.
PSO2
Design, conduct, analyse, and communicate biochemical research with sound analytical skills.
PSO3
Apply biochemistry to medicine, biotechnology, and pharmaceuticals—understanding roles in health and disease.
PSO4
Be career-ready for academia, research, industry, and healthcare (clinical biochemistry, lab management, pharma R&D).
PSO5
Integrate interdisciplinary knowledge from molecular biology, immunology, endocrinology, and related fields.
