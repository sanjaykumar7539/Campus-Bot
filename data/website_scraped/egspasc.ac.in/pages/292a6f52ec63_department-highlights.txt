Microbiology Department Highlights
Students are exposed to real world applicability of microorganisms through regular industrial visits and educational trips to institutions.
The department organized national, level seminar on ‘microvision’ work shop, industrial visit, out rich programme, guest lecturer etc.
The Department maintains a vibrant atmosphere by regularly organizing events like Science Fest, Youth Flair, Fresher’s welcome, Farewell and Teachers day celebrations etc.
Our students have been featured in various Biotechnological, Food and Pharmaceutical companies like Tata Beverages, Ranbaxy, Panacea Biotech, Nestle, Dr Lal PathLabs, Verka, Multi-speciality hospitals and various research and educational institutes.
