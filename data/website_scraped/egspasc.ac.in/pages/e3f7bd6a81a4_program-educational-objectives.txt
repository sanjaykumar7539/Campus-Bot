##
Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO1
Provide in-depth understanding of core concepts in chemical sciences and tools to organise and interpret chemical information.
PEO2
Expose students to modern experimental techniques and instrumentation; inculcate safe laboratory practices and research ethics.
PEO3
Develop expertise in the interdisciplinary nature of Chemistry by integrating Mathematics, Physics, and allied domains to solve diverse chemical problems.
PEO4
Build effective scientific communication skills for disseminating ideas and results in written and oral forms.
PEO5
Foster professionalism, teamwork, and adherence to ethical principles in academic and industrial environments.
