Program Outcomes (POs)
Program Outcomes describe what students are expected to know or be able to do by graduation.
##
Program Outcome
PO1
Disciplinary knowledge in commerce and management.
PO2
Effective written and oral communication using appropriate media.
PO3
Critical thinking to evaluate evidence and formulate arguments.
PO4
Problem solving in unfamiliar business contexts.
PO5
Analytical reasoning; synthesize data and draw valid conclusions.
PO6
Research skills: problem definition, hypothesis testing and reporting.
PO7
Teamwork and collaboration in diverse groups.
PO8
Scientific/quantitative reasoning in business decisions.
PO9
Reflective thinking with self-awareness and social sensibility.
PO10
Information/digital literacy; effective use of ICT tools.
PO11
Self-directed learning and project management.
PO12
Multicultural competence and global perspective.
PO13
Moral and ethical awareness; responsible conduct.
PO14
Leadership readiness and people management.
PO15
Lifelong learning for career and personal development.
