Department of Visual Communication
About the Department
Visual communication is the practice of using visual elements like images, symbols, typography, and design to convey messages, ideas, and information. It is a powerful and efficient way to communicate, as it can quickly convey complex concepts and evoke emotions, transcending language barriers. The field includes a wide range of media, from traditional methods like drawing and photography to digital tools such as graphic design software, animation, and video.
The department of visual communication was established in 2018 at E.G.S.Pillay College. Since its inception, the department has been dedicated to nurturing creative minds with strong foundations in media, design, photography, and communication. Over the years, it has grown into a vibrant centre for innovation, practical learning, and industry-focused training, empowering students to excel in various creative fields.
Scope of Objective
Film and television:
Working as a director, animator, editor, cinematographer, or producer in film, documentary, or television production.
Digital media and web design:
Designing user-friendly interfaces, websites, and engaging content for online platforms and social media.
Graphic design:
Working as a graphic designer, layout artist, or illustrator for a variety of clients and projects.
Multimedia and animation:
Creating 2D and 3D animations and multimedia content for various applications.
