Program Outcomes (POs)
On successful completion of the programme, graduates are expected to achieve the following:
##
Program Outcomes
1
Produces industry-ready graduates with strong laboratory skills, modern instrumentation expertise and problem-solving abilities.
2
Cultivates research-oriented scholars through active projects, publications and hands-on exposure to advanced chemical sciences.
3
Promotes environmental and green chemistry awareness, preparing students for sustainable scientific practices.
4
Encourages innovation and entrepreneurship, guiding students to convert scientific ideas into real-world applications.
