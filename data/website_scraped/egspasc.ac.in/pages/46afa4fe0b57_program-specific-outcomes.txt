Program Specific Outcomes
Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
Demonstrate expertise in designing and constructing creative garments using advanced techniques in pattern making and draping.
Utilize modern fashion software and CAD tools to develop professional design portfolios and manufacturing layouts.
Apply knowledge of textile materials and sustainability to create commercially viable and eco-friendly fashion solutions.
Exhibit readiness for professional roles in fashion merchandising, styling, and costume design for the media industry.
