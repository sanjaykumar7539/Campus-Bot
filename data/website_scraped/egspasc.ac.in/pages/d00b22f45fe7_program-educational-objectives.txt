Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
##
Program Educational Objectives
PEO1
Obtain in-depth knowledge of software and hardware techniques to build a compact foundation for continuing education, innovation, and research.
PEO2
Engage in IT professions locally and globally, contributing ethically to competent and professional practices.
PEO3
Develop skills in modern tools for critical problem solving and analyzing industrial and societal requirements.
PEO4
Train graduates in diversified, applied areas with analysis, design, and synthesis to create novel solutions for current industrial and societal needs.
PEO5
Nurture leadership at appropriate levels to address issues in a responsive, ethical, and innovative manner.
