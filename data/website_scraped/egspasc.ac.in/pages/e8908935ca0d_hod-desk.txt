Department of Commerce (CA)
Welcome to the Department of B.Com – Computer Applications. Our programme integrates
core commerce with hands-on computer application skills to prepare graduates who can
analyse, design and digitalise business processes with responsibility and impact.
Students gain strong conceptual knowledge in accounting, finance, law and markets,
alongside practical exposure to programming, databases, ERP and MIS through projects,
internships and industry collaborations.
We strive to nurture ethical professionals, entrepreneurs and researchers who contribute
to business, economy and society.
