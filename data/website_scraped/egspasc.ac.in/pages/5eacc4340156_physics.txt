About the Department
Physics is the scientific study of matter and energy, investigating how the fundamental constituents of the universe interact and behave. It seeks to understand the physical world from the smallest subatomic particles to the entire universe, focusing on concepts like motion, forces, energy, and space-time. Physics is considered a fundamental science, as its principles are the foundation for many other scientific disciplines and technological advancements.
Vision
To impart Physics knowledge to rural and semi-urban students so they become
educated individuals equipped for life, capable of contributing to the scientific
and technological development of society.
Mission
To nurture enthusiasm, strong interest, and passion towards learning Physics.
To train students in both theoretical and experimental Physics to develop
critical thinking, logical reasoning, and application skills.
To improve programme quality through excellence in teaching–learning techniques.
To prepare graduates who are skilled to meet the needs and challenges of a dynamic society.
To promote current scientific knowledge and achievements through active research involvement.
