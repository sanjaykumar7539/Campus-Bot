Department of Computer Applications
Introduction
BCA full form is Bachelor of Computer Applications. It is a three-year undergraduate degree program that helps students learn to solve challenging problems in today’s fast-moving technology world. A Bachelor of Computer Application degree is the core of Computer Science in today’s world.
This degree is for those who want to study computer science, software engineering, information technology, information security, and networking technology. The degree focuses on the design, development, and deployment of software systems with a special focus on the design and analysis of software security.
Vision
To disseminate quality education in Computer Science and related fields, especially to rural
students, enabling them to build a strong foundation and confidently face the challenges of
the IT world.
Mission
To adopt improved teaching methodologies through continuous faculty training and upgradation
programmes.
To facilitate teachers in imparting computer education and its practical applications
effectively.
To motivate students to achieve excellent results and prepare them for independent growth and
progressive careers with long-term professional employability.
To attain leadership in planning and resource management in order to enhance the quality of
technical education.
To cultivate values and vibrancy within the department that support the development activities
of the college.
To make significant contributions in key areas such as faculty development, modern laboratories,
and advanced learning resources.
