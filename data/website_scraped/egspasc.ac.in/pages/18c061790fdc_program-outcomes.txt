Program Outcomes (POs)
B.Sc. Nutrition & Dietetics — Program Outcomes
##
Outcome
PO1
Critical thinking to apply nutrition & dietetics knowledge to prevent/treat diseases.
PO2
Design/development of solutions and innovative food products for societal benefit.
PO3
Problem analysis using fundamentals of clinical nutrition, physiology, food science, and biochemistry.
PO4
Modern tool usage to compute nutritional needs with awareness of limitations.
PO5
Environment & sustainability awareness in local and global contexts.
PO6
Function effectively as an individual and member of diverse teams.
PO7
Professional ethics and responsibility in nutrition and dietetics practice.
PO8
Self-directed and lifelong learning for continual professional development.
M.Sc. Food Science & Nutrition — Program Outcomes
Comprehensive and updated understanding of food science and nutrition concepts.
Assess nutritional status and plan diets using appropriate methodologies.
Work in food businesses/industries and apply food safety management tools.
Apply nutrition in clinical interventions, health promotion, food service, and processing.
Undertake systematic research in food science and nutrition.
