Infrastructure
E.G.S. Pillay Arts and Science College provides a modern academic environment with well-planned infrastructure and advanced facilities.
Main Academic Block (Houses Arts, Science, and Commerce Departments)
Laboratory Block (Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, and Biotechnology Labs)
Library Block with Digital Library and Reading Halls
Administrative Block (Principal’s Office, Administrative Office, and Meeting Halls)
Auditorium and Seminar Hall for Academic and Cultural Events
Cafeteria and Food Court
The college buildings are designed with spacious classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, conference halls, staff rooms, and department offices — all meeting the standards prescribed by Bharathidasan University and the Tamil Nadu Government.
The campus is surrounded by lush greenery providing a peaceful and inspiring atmosphere for learning. The College also provides separate, well-maintained hostels for boys and girls with essential amenities, including:
Study and Reading Halls
Recreation Rooms with Television
Indoor Games and Fitness Facilities
Wi-Fi Connectivity in Common Areas
Dining Halls with Hygienic Food
Medical and Counseling Support
24x7 Security and Power Backup
Computer Facilities
Computer Laboratories equipped with over 200 modern systems with high-speed Internet access.
Systems with latest configurations (i3/i5/i7 processors) and UPS backup.
Operating Systems: Windows 10/11 and Linux.
Centralized Server Room with data management facilities.
Smart Classrooms with ICT-enabled teaching aids.
Internet and Wi-Fi
The entire campus is Wi-Fi enabled with a high-speed 1 Gbps internet connection.
Students and faculty have seamless access to online learning platforms, digital resources, and research databases from classrooms, library, and hostels.
Library
Spacious central library with more than 30,000 volumes and 100+ journals.
Digital Library with access to e-books, e-journals, and research databases.
Reading halls and reference sections for students and staff.
