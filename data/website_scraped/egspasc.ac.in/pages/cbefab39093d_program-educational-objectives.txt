Professional Competence

PEO2
Technological Adaptability
: Graduates will adapt to emerging technologies in Big Data, AI, and Analytics.

Ethical Practice
: Graduates will uphold professional ethics and standards in handling sensitive data.
PEO4
Lifelong Learning
: Graduates will engage in continuous learning and professional development in the field of Data Science.
