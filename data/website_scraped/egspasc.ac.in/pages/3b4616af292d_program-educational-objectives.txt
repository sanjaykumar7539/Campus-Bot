Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
B.Sc. Mathematics – PEOs
##
PEO
PEO1
Be prepared for successful careers at local, national, and international levels (analyst, quality controller, research assistant, etc.).
PEO2
Exhibit leadership, lifelong learning, teamwork, and ethical attitude for a successful professional career.
PEO3
Formulate, investigate, and analyze real-life problems with scientific rigor and ethics.
PEO4
Engage in professional activities that enhance stature and contribute to society.
PEO5
Succeed in higher education in science and management, if pursued.
M.Sc. Mathematics – PEOs
Explore significant opportunities in banking, insurance, government, consultancy, teaching, defense, industry, research, and entrepreneurship.
Gain recognition as individuals/teams with specialized knowledge to solve problems in pure, applied, and computational mathematics.
Demonstrate leadership in diverse contexts via inter/multidisciplinary learning.
Adopt continuous learning to master new tools and technologies.
Uphold values and ethics, contributing through critical thinking and mathematical competence.
