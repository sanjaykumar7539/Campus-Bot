Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
##
Program Educational Objectives
PEO1
Prepare graduates to exhibit excellence, critical thinking, creativity, inventiveness, and self-motivation for lifelong learning in interdisciplinary/multidisciplinary environments.
PEO2
Produce globally acceptable professionals for government, corporate, and research organizations with skills for entrepreneurial pursuits.
PEO3
Enable graduates to demonstrate technical competence in Physics and develop solutions to complex problems.
PEO4
Foster ethical leadership and teamwork towards fulfilling individual and organizational goals.
PEO5
Engage graduates in professional pursuits that enhance personal achievement and serve society at large.
PEO6
Impart research and innovation skills to develop competent researchers.
