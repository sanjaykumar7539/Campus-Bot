Fashion Technology & Costume Designing
About the Department
Bachelor of Fashion Technology and Costume Designing
is a dynamic, multidisciplinary program that seamlessly blends artistic creativity with essential technical and business skills, preparing students comprehensively for the demands of the global fashion industry. It offers a complete understanding of the fashion process, starting from the initial design concept all the way to the final garment production.
A significant portion of the curriculum is dedicated to core technical skills like manual and computer-aided pattern making (CAD), mastering the art of drafting basic patterns, and the nuance of fabric draping on forms. The program also integrates modern fashion technology and explores the vital business aspects of the industry, such as merchandising, marketing, trend forecasting, and quality control standards. Ultimately, the course is structured to develop highly job-ready skills, providing numerous opportunities for internships and real-world exposure to production houses, design firms, and the broader creative arts sector.
Scope
Fashion Technology
integrates manufacturing processes, utilizing tools like 3D printing and CAD software to produce innovative, sustainable, and mass-market appealing apparel and textiles.
Costume Designing
is an artistic field involving extensive research to create specific outfits for characters in film, theatre, or television productions, focusing on narrative requirements and historical context rather than general market trends.
Career Scope
in costume design typically leads to jobs in the entertainment industry, working with directors, stylists, and makeup artists to bring characters to life visually.
Vision
Provide rigorous standards for design knowledge, illustration skills, industrial and
professional behaviour; contribute to a designer society through teaching in classrooms,
fashion studios, and software-led design; and foster a collaborative, collegial,
and cooperative learning community of diverse students.
Mission
Be a core of excellence in fashion designing.
Produce creative design projects and valuable critiques.
Implement global standards and encourage innovation through quality education.
Develop professionals who contribute to society with integrity and commitment.
Foster student innovation and hands-on capability building.
