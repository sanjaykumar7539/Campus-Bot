List of Clubs and Cells
E.G.S. Pillay Arts and Science College (Autonomous) maintains a comprehensive spectrum of student clubs, statutory committees, and specialized cells dedicated to academic enrichment, student welfare, social outreach, environmental consciousness, and professional empowerment.
