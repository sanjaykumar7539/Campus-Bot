##
UG Programme Specific Outcomes
PSO1
Acquire knowledge in hardware & software aspects of Computer Science and programming.
PSO2
Understand software development methodologies; analyze, design, and develop applications for real-life problems.
PSO3
Collaborate and communicate effectively with peers in IT/ITES industries.
PSO4
Adapt to dynamic technical environments for the growth of the IT industry.
PSO5
Transfer learned skills to provide innovative, ethical, and socially responsible solutions.
PG Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
PG Programme Specific Outcomes
Post-graduates demonstrate advanced competency after successful completion of the M.Sc (IT) programme.
Understand how technical developments are achieved and managed.
Enhance critical thinking, code-writing, and configuring technical tools.
Prepare for a wide variety of careers across IT industries with a research-oriented mindset.
Develop strong analytical, critical thinking, and experimental skills.
PSO6
Understand how technological advances impact society and the social, legal, ethical, and cultural ramifications of computing.
