Department of Commerce-Business Process Service & Professional Accounting
Overview of the Department
The Department of Commerce in Business Process Services (BPS) was started in
June 2021
.
The program is designed to enlighten minds toward Business Process Services and meet the need for
graduates who can be self-employed or join BPS organizations. It began with an
MoU with TCS
in collaboration with
Bharathidasan University
, and its syllabus is approved by our Board of
Studies and several universities.
The program equips students with skills to manage business processes—
Banking, Insurance, Financial
Services, Capital Markets
—alongside strong fundamentals. Employability in the BPS industry is
promising. The inaugural batch (2021–2022) enrolled
34 students
with dedicated faculty and a
Head of Department leading the program.
