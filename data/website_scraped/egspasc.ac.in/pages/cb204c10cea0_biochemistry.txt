Department of Biochemistry
About the Department
The Department of Biochemistry was established in 1998 under the Tutelage of Chev.Dr.G.S.pillay Ayya and offered B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D., in Biochemistry; from then it is imparting quality education in the field of life sciences. It has excellent infrastructural facilities to cater to the needs of the students. The department has organized workshops and seminars in the yester years in association with other related departments. It has got many of its alumni well placed in India and Abroad.
Scope of Biochemistry
Biochemistry is one of the branches of chemistry which deals with the chemical basis of life in plants and animals.
It is used in clinical diagnosis, manufacture of various biological products treatment of diseases, in nutrition, agriculture, etc.
It tries to explain life in terms of biochemical reactions, including other sciences as anatomy, medicine, agriculture, pharmacy, etc.
Vision
To be a centre of excellence that advances understanding of biochemical and molecular
events—forming a keystone of modern biomedical sciences—and enhances the quality of life.
Mission
Provide broad-based knowledge of biochemistry at molecular and scientific levels.
Uphold ethical teaching and a supportive, inclusive learning environment.
Promote innovation and novel approaches in PG projects and doctoral research.
Offer rigorous experimental exposure through industrial visits and internships.
Encourage high-quality student activities with mutual respect and care at work.
