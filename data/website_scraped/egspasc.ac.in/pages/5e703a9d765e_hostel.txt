Hostel Facilities
Hostel Administration
The hostel administration is managed by the Secretary with assistance from the Warden and Deputy Wardens. Residential Lecturers are available to provide guidance and counseling to students.
A separate mess operates inside the hostel, providing quality and nutritious meals, both vegetarian and non-vegetarian.
Hostel Admission
Students can obtain the hostel admission application form from the hostel office. Applicants must meet the Warden with a parent or guardian and pay the prescribed hostel admission fees.
Parent or guardian presence is mandatory at the time of admission to ensure proper guidance and verification.
Hostel & Mess Facilities
Separate, well-furnished hostels for boys and girls
Fine dining facilities serving nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals
First-aid and healthcare facilities with an appointed college doctor
Recreational areas and study spaces within the hostels
Five Deputy Wardens dedicated to student welfare
Safe drinking water and hygienic living conditions
Staff & Guest Accommodations
Faculty may reside with their families in the staff quarters provided on campus. A guest house is also available for visitors to the college, ensuring comfortable accommodations for both staff and guests.
