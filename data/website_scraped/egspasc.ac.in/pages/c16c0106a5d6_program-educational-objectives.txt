##
Program Educational Objectives
1
To apply biological systems for developing innovative products and technologies.
2
To enhance human health through advanced diagnostics, therapeutics, and vaccines.
3
To improve agricultural productivity using sustainable and eco-friendly methods.
4
To promote research in genetic engineering, molecular biology, and bioinformatics.
5
To develop skilled professionals for biotechnology industries and research institutions.
