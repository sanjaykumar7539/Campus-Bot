##
Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO1
Graduates of the Programme will be successful engineers in the field of Computer Science who solve industry and social problems.
PEO2
Graduates of the Programme will be engineering professionals, innovators or entrepreneurs engaged in technology development, deployment, or engineering system implementation in industry.
PEO3
Graduates of the Programme will pursue higher studies in engineering or management and follow career paths in teaching or research.
