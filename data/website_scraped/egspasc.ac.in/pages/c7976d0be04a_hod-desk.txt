Mr. T. Manivannan, MCA., M.Phil., SET., (Ph.D.)
Head of Department & Assistant Professor
Department of Computer Applications
Welcome to the Department of Computer Applications. As the Head of the
Department, it gives me immense pleasure to introduce our programmes
designed to equip students with the skills and knowledge required to thrive
in today’s digital world. The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
grooms students to become proficient software developers and IT professionals,
covering programming languages, database management, web development, and more.
We emphasize experiential learning through projects, internships, and
industry collaborations. Our state-of-the-art labs enable students to apply
theoretical concepts to real-world scenarios. We foster an environment of
innovation, creativity, and critical thinking that prepares learners to be
leaders in the ever-evolving field of technology.
The department inculcates strong technical values to help students advance
in their careers and face competitive real-world challenges. Our faculty
delivers high-quality learning experiences using modern computing resources,
supporting Outcome Based Education (OBE).
We prepare data-driven professionals with skills in building intelligent
systems, analytics, and visualization. The department maintains excellent,
regularly upgraded lab facilities, and organizes interactive lectures,
Faculty Development Programmes, webinars, and seminars with experts from
academia and industry for holistic development.
We look forward to welcoming you to our vibrant academic community.
