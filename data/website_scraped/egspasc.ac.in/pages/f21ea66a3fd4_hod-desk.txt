Mr. V. Senthil Murugan, M.Sc., M.Phil., (Ph.D.)
Head of Department & Assistant Professor
Department of Physics
It is a pleasure to head the Department of Physics at E. G. S. Pillay Arts & Science
College (Autonomous). The department offers B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programmes and is
supported by a team of highly experienced and motivated faculty members. Our students are
well-prepared for global competition through hands-on exposure to current technologies.
Students consistently secure top positions and are placed in leading companies such as
Bright Star, Wipro, Accenture, TCS, Zealous Services, HCL, Tech Mahindra, IDBI, and HGS.
We facilitate Personality Development, Communication classes, and preparation for
competitive exams. Co-curricular and extra-curricular activities thrive at institute level
with active participation in intra/inter-college events.
Faculty and students also take initiatives for social causes through various clubs. Turning
a student into a good citizen is the prime aim of the Department of Physics.
