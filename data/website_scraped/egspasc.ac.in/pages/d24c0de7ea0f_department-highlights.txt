Highlights – Business Administration (BBA & MBA)
Experienced Faculty:
Look for professors with real-world experience in business, not just academics.
Industry Connections:
A strong MBA department will have connections with companies, allowing for internships and job opportunities.
Hands-on Learning:
This could include case studies, simulations, and projects that mimic real-world business challenges.
Specialized Tracks:
Some MBA programs offer tracks in specific areas like marketing, finance, human resources and logistics.
Student Clubs and Organizations:
These offer networking, leadership opportunities, and a chance to explore different business interests.
The best fit depends on your goals—speak with our advisors to tailor your path.
