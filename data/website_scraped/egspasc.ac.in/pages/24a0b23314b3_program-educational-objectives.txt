Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
##
Program Educational Objectives
PEO1
Demonstrate proficiency in software development, including designing, implementing, testing, and maintaining software solutions for diverse applications. (Technical Expertise & Application Development)
PEO2
Apply programming languages, algorithms, and data structures to develop innovative and effective solutions. (Technical Expertise & Application Development)
PEO3
Analyze complex problems and design, develop, and evaluate computer-based solutions that satisfy specified requirements and constraints. (Technical Expertise & Application Development)
PEO4
Build successful careers in the software and IT industry, and/or pursue entrepreneurship, research, or higher education. (Career Success & Professional Development)
PEO5
Exhibit professionalism, ethical conduct, and strong communication skills to collaborate effectively and contribute meaningfully to teams. (Career Success & Professional Development)
PEO6
Adapt to emerging technologies and engage in lifelong learning for continuous upskilling and relevance. (Career Success & Professional Development)
PEO7
Recognize the societal impact of computing solutions and consider their ethical implications in practice. (Societal Impact & Responsibility)
PEO8
Identify and address societal challenges by applying computer applications knowledge and skills. (Societal Impact & Responsibility)
PEO9
Communicate effectively with diverse stakeholders—including technical and non-technical audiences—through appropriate formats and media. (Societal Impact & Responsibility)
