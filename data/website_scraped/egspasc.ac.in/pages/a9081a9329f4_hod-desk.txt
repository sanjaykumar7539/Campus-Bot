Department of Data Science
Welcome to the Department of Data Science. B.Sc Data Science is a fascinating full-time course that combines elements of Computer Science, Business Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence. This field is all about utilizing Statistics, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, and related tools to gain insights from real-world data.
Our goal is to equip students with practical skills in areas like data mining, machine learning, big data analytics, and data visualization, preparing them for data-driven careers in a rapidly evolving technological landscape.
