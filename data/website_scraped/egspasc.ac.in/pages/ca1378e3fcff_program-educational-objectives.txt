Program Educational Objectives
Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
No.
PEO1
Technical Proficiency:
Graduates will practice Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in a broad range of industries, demonstrating technical competence and solving real-world problems.
PEO2
Professional Growth:
Graduates will pursue higher education or professional development in AI/ML and related fields to adapt to technological advancements.
PEO3
Ethical Leadership:
Graduates will demonstrate leadership qualities and ethical responsibilities in their professional careers, contributing to the betterment of society.
PEO4
Collaboration and Innovation:
Graduates will work effectively in multidisciplinary teams and innovate solutions using AI technologies.
