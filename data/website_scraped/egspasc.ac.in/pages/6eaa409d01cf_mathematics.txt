About the Department
The Department is one among the department serving the academic needs of the Institution since 2006. The PG Programme was established in the year 2009. It offers B.Sc., M.Sc., and research program. The department is taken care of functions efficiently by Head of the Department
Dr. R.Mekala supported by eight faculty members.
We are committed to strengthening foundational and advanced mathematical skills, encouraging
research attitude, and enabling students to apply mathematics to real-world problems across
science, technology, and industry.
Vision
To provide students with individualized help in their studies and develop a program whereby
students may obtain tutoring as needed to successfully complete their mathematics courses.
Mission
Offer academic challenges with appropriate support to help students succeed.
Make students employable by involving them in various competitive examinations.
Motivate students to present papers in national and international seminars and conferences.
