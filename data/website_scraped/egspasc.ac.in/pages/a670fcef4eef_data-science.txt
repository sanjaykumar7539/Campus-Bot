Department of Data Science
Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) in Data Science is an undergraduate program that provides a foundation in mathematics, statistics, and computer science to analyze and interpret complex data. It equips students with practical skills in areas like data mining, machine learning, big data analytics, and data visualization, preparing them for data-driven careers.
Course Offered
B.Sc Data Science
This undergraduate program provides foundation knowledge in the fields and prepares students for future careers.
Eligibility
A pass in +2, Compulsory Mathematics with/without Computer Science.
