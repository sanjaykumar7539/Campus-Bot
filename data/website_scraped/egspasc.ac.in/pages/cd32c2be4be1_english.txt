About the Department of English
The Department of English fosters strong communication, critical thinking, and literary analysis skills through a comprehensive curriculum.
It encourages creativity, cultural awareness, and academic excellence through seminars, workshops, and innovative learning methods.
Students are trained in research, language proficiency, and soft skills needed for global careers.
The department is committed to shaping confident graduates ready for diverse professional fields.
Objectives of the Department
To develop strong linguistic and literary competence.
To promote critical and creative thinking through diverse literary texts.
To equip students with communication and interpersonal skills for modern workplaces.
To prepare students for competitive exams, research, and professional careers.
