TCS & Department Collaboration
The Department of Computer Science with Cognitive System collaborates with Tata Consultancy Services (TCS),
a global leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions. This partnership bridges academic learning
with industry expectations, equipping students with the latest technological skills and practical knowledge.
