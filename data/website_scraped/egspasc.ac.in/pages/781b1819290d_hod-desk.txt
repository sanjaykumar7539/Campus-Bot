Mrs. S. Sudharani, M.Sc., M.Phil.
Assistant Professor & Head
Department of Visual Communication
Visual Communication is offered with on objective to train and prepare highly skilled and professional man power to the media industry and also provide opportunity for self-employment. Our Lecturers trains and enables every student to learn the potential skills needed in the media field which greatly helps them to face all the challenges.
Throughout the curriculum students will receive the complete knowledge of Visual communication by undergoing regular assignments, practical, live projects, workshops and internships. The department provides wonderful ambience for learning and state-of-the-art facilities like Drawing Studio, Multimedia lab, Green Matte Studio, Sound Recording Theater, Edit suite.
The course transforms students into one of the best media professional, film makers, videographers, photographers, sound engineers, video editors, web designers, animators and graphic designers etc. My best wishes and greetings to all the students.
