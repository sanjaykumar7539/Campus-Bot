B.Sc. Biochemistry — PEOs
##
Objective
1
To apply chemical principles to understand biological problems and develop innovative solutions.
2
To analyse biological samples and diagnose various health conditions.
3
To advance drug development and therapeutic strategies by studying disease mechanisms.
4
To promote research in areas like enzymology, metabolism, and molecular biology.
5
To develop skilled professionals for the healthcare, research, and biochemical and chemical industries.
