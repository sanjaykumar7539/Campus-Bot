Department of Business Administration (BBA & MBA)
Overview
The Department of Business Administration is the heart of our business school—where students
learn the fundamentals of running successful organizations. Our BBA program builds a strong
toolkit across finance, marketing, HR, operations, and analytics, while our MBA elevates
professional practice for graduates and working professionals who seek leadership roles or
entrepreneurial pathways.
The MBA offers focused pathways in
Finance
,
Human Resource Management
and
Logistics
—covering financial analysis & risk, talent management & compliance,
and supply chain optimization from procurement to distribution. Together, BBA and MBA cultivate
decision-making, leadership, and the ability to solve complex business problems with impact.
Vision
To provide premier business education and develop students into new-generation
leaders who adapt to emerging business practices.
Mission
To provide competency-driven education and inculcate best management practices.
To offer professional development and career opportunities in emerging business areas.
To make every student globally fit for business with human values and social concern.
