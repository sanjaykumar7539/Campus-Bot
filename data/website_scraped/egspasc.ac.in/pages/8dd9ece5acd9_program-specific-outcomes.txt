Programme Specific Outcomes
PSO1
Develop professional skills to analyze concepts and apply technology in banking and other businesses.
PSO2
Cope with recent trends in commerce and business processes with emphasis on employability skills.
PSO3
Take responsibility, work constructively, and lead within the community with teamwork and cooperation.
PSO4
Communicate effectively in both verbal and written forms.
PSO5
Develop broad business skills and knowledge to meet current and future expectations of business, industry, and the economy at national and global levels.
