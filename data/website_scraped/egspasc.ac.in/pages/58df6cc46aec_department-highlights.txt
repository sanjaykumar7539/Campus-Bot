B.Sc. Data Science Highlights
B.Sc Data Science is a fascinating 3-year full-time course that combines elements of Computer Science, Business Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence. This field is all about utilizing Statistics, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, and related tools to gain insights from real-world data.
