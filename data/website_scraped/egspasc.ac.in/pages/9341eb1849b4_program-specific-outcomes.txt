Programme Specific Outcomes
PSO1
Contribute effectively in Mathematical Statistics, Differential & Integral Calculus.
PSO2
Demonstrate competence in Vector Calculus, Transform Techniques, and Analysis.
PSO3
Build strong foundations in Real Analysis, Complex Analysis, and Linear Algebra.
M.Sc. Mathematics – PSOs
Understand and apply concepts in Algebra, Analysis, Optimization, Differential Equations, Computational Techniques, Engineering, Finance, and Actuarial Science.
Handle advanced techniques; design algorithms to solve a variety of real-life problems.
Adapt to changing scientific environments for sustainable development using mathematical tools.
PSO4
Develop research skills via seminars and dissertation work.
