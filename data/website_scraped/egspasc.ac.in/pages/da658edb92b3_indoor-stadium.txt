Indoor Stadium
E.G.S. Pillay Arts and Science College Indoor Stadium
The Indoor Stadium at
Edayathangudy G.S. Pillay Arts and Science College
stands as one of the most prominent facilities on campus, designed to promote physical fitness, teamwork, and a balanced student lifestyle.
It provides state-of-the-art amenities to host a variety of indoor games, tournaments, and training sessions throughout the academic year.
Facilities Available
Spacious, multi-purpose indoor court with synthetic flooring
Facilities for Badminton, Table Tennis, Volleyball, and Basketball practice
Separate areas for Yoga, Aerobics, and Fitness activities
Dedicated seating area for spectators and participants
Modern lighting and ventilation for all-weather sports activities
Changing rooms and locker facilities for students and staff
Regular inter-college tournaments and training sessions
Purpose & Vision
The indoor stadium serves as a hub for sports excellence and holistic development.
It encourages students to actively participate in physical activities alongside academics, nurturing both discipline and team spirit.
The facility also supports coaching camps and fitness programs organized by the Department of Physical Education.
Accessibility
The stadium is accessible to all students and faculty members during college hours.
It remains one of the most vibrant areas on campus, frequently hosting sports meets, cultural events, and student recreation sessions.
