Placements
Strong institute–industry interaction; professional career counseling; dedicated placement cell.
Pre-placement training; collaborations with industry giants; alumni networking support.
High placement success rate. Recruiters include: Capgemini, CTS, HP, IBM, Infosys, Wipro, Tech Mahindra, TCS, Hexaware, etc.
