Department of Biotechnology
About the Department
Biotechnology is the use of living organisms, biological systems, or their components to develop products and technologies for specific purposes. This multidisciplinary field combines biology, chemistry, and engineering to create useful innovations in areas like medicine, agriculture, and industry, and can include both ancient practices and modern techniques. Since its inception, the Department has contributed a lot in terms of teaching, learning and research. We have a team of faculties who contributes much to the development of the potentials of students. Department enhances the knowledge base of students and creates research curiosity among students generating highly skilled human resource. The autonomy brings into force industry and research oriented syllabus and hour based curriculum helps the students to excel their knowledge towards innovate, incubate ideas and nurture the needs of society.
Scope of Biotechnology
Biotechnology offers wide career opportunities in healthcare, agriculture, environment and industry.
It provides skills to develop innovative technologies like vaccines, biofuels, and gene-based solutions.
Students can work in R&D labs, pharma companies, biotech industries, and government research institutes.
This field supports sustainable agriculture through tissue culture, biofertilizers, and improved crop varieties.
Biotechnology graduates have strong scope for higher studies, research, and global job opportunities.
Overview
The Faculty of Biotechnology bridges scientific discovery and real-world applications.
Our programs equip students with the knowledge, skills, and expertise to solve complex
problems and drive innovation across healthcare, agriculture, environment, and industry.
Vision
Students learn to apply the principles of biology, medicine, and science with
problem-solving and critical thinking to a broad spectrum of problems in biotechnology
and medicine. The Department is enriched with quality journals and periodicals.
Mission
Producing quality technocrats with strong knowledge in Biological Sciences.
Strengthening research activities within the department.
Encouraging consultancy and extension services involving students and faculty.
Preparing students for placements and higher studies; building industry-ready talent.
Infrastructure
The labs are well equipped with PCR, Gel Documentation system, Flame Photometers,
Horizontal & Vertical Laminar Airflow Chambers, Spectrophotometer, Electrophoresis
apparatus, Cooling & Clinical Centrifuges, Incubators, Hot Air Oven, Shaking
Incubator, Deep Freezer, UV Transilluminator, and other essential instruments for
molecular biology workflows.
