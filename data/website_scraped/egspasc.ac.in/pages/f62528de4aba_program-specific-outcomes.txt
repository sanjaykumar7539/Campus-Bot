Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
##
Programme Specific Outcomes
PSO1
Core Competence:
Demonstrate strong grasp of chemical principles and perform standard laboratory experiments safely and effectively.
PSO2
Critical & Analytical Thinking:
Analyse complex chemical problems, evaluate information, synthesise insights, and communicate findings clearly.
PSO3
Problem Solving:
Integrate chemical theory with mathematical/technical skills to model systems, interpret data, and propose solutions.
PSO4
Scientific Reasoning:
Apply the scientific method, test hypotheses, use appropriate statistics, and employ computational tools/models.
PSO5
Research Skills:
Frame research questions, conduct literature reviews, design and execute studies, and disseminate outcomes responsibly.
PSO6
Self-Directed & Lifelong Learning:
Set learning goals, adapt to new contexts, collaborate effectively, and pursue continuous professional development.
