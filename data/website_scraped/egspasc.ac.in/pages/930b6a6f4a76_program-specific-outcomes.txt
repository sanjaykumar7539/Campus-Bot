Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
##
Programme Specific Outcomes
PSO1
Functional Knowledge:
Demonstrate understanding of Marketing, Finance, HR, and Operations to contribute across business domains.
PSO2
Problem-Solving & Decision-Making:
Analyze business problems, design solutions, and make informed decisions (with advanced capability at MBA level).
PSO3
Communication & Professionalism:
Present ideas effectively; lead and manage teams with strong interpersonal skills.
PSO4
Leadership & Teamwork:
Exhibit strategic thinking, effective management practices, and collaborative leadership.
PSO5
Entrepreneurship:
Apply entrepreneurial mindset and tools to start and grow ventures.
PSO6
Global Perspective:
Understand international business practices and operate in global contexts.
