Department of Fashion Technology & Costume Designing
Welcome to the Department of Fashion Technology & Costume Designing. Students are mentored by qualified faculty in all aspects of fashion—brainstorming, designing, illustration, pattern making, and surface embellishments—transforming concepts into products.
Our undergraduate program fuels creativity and entrepreneurial spirit. Students actively participate in contests and earn accolades. Beyond fashion activities, our community contributes to social causes—including an award-winning film project on gender perspectives submitted to international film festivals.
