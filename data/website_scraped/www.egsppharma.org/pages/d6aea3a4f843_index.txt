D.Pharm
Eligibility Criteria for admission:
A Pass in H.Sc with Physics, Chemistry & Biology or Mathematics or Botany & Zoology.

B.Pharm
Eligibility Criteria for admission for First year:
A Pass in H.Sc with 40% marks in Physics, Chemistry & Biology or Mathematics or Botany & Zoology. 35% marks for SC/ST category.

A pass in D.Pharm course
Duration: 4 Years for Regular
Duration: 3 Years for Lateral Entry

Pharm.D
Eligibility Criteria for Admission :
A Pass in H.Sc with 35% marks in Physics, Chemistry & Biology or Mathematics or Botany & Zoology.
