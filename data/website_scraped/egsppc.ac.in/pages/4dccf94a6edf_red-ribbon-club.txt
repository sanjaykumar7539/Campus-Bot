Red Ribbon Club
About Red Ribbon Club
The Red Ribbon Club organizes awareness camps on AIDS and blood donation in village areas, aiming to educate and empower youth.
01.
Goal of RRC
Equipping youth with correct information on HIV/AIDS prevention, treatment, care, and support to create an HIV/AIDS-free India.
02.
Main Objectives of RRC
To prepare youth as peer educators and agents of change.
To serve as forums for students to participate in the fight against HIV/AIDS.
To promote voluntary blood donation among youth.
To motivate youth and build their capacity as peer educators.
To counsel students regarding various preventive measures for their health.
03.
Regular Activities of RRC
Workshops on AIDS awareness.
College-level elocution and singing competitions.
Blood donation camps.
