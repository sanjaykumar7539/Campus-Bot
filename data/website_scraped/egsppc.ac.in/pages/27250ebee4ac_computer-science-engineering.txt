Computer Science Engineering
Computer Engineering
Established in 2011, the Department of Computer Engineering offers a diploma course designed to provide students with a thorough grounding in computer engineering. The program prepares students to face future challenges, whether by joining the high-technology workforce or pursuing advanced studies.
The department provides a solid foundation in both theoretical and practical aspects of computer science, integrating emerging technologies and hands-on learning experiences. This approach ensures that graduates are well-equipped to meet the evolving demands of the computer industry.
The department’s commitment to excellence has significantly impacted the field, with graduates being highly sought after by employers worldwide.
Vision
To be a centre of academic excellence in computer engineering, evolving to meet the challenges of the 21st century. We aim to foster the development of world-class computer engineers who are technically proficient, innovative, and passionate about addressing real-world challenges, creating sustainable solutions, and driving technological advancement with an entrepreneurial spirit.
Mission
To cultivate excellent computer engineers with strong analytical and management skills in computer hardware, networking, software, and entrepreneurship. Our mission is to equip students with the latest trends, technologies, and socio-ethical understanding to enable them to excel in their professions and contribute meaningfully to the industry and society.
