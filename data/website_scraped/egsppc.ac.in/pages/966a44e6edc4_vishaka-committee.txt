Vishaka Committee
About Vishaka Committee
The Vishaka Committee is dedicated to creating a safe and supportive environment for all female students and staff. The committee aims to raise social awareness, educate on women’s welfare laws, promote health and hygiene, and address issues related to sexual harassment.
Objectives of the Vishaka Committee
Ensure a secure and conducive working and learning environment for all female students and staff, including both teaching and non-teaching members.
Foster awareness about gender-related issues, including discrimination, and educate the community about these challenges.
Develop programs and initiatives to boost the self-confidence and self-esteem of female students and staff.
Provide information and education about legal protections and rights related to women’s welfare.
Emphasize the importance of health and hygiene through various awareness and education campaigns.
Increase awareness and provide resources to combat sexual harassment, ensuring a safe environment for all.
Encourage active participation of female students and staff in sports, NSS, and NCC activities to promote physical fitness and personal growth.
