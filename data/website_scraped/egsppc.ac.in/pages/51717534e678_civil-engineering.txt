Civil Engineering
The Department of Civil Engineering, established in 2011 with an initial intake of 60 students, boasts well-qualified faculty and technical staff. Equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, the department meets the demands of modern societal and industrial needs. It actively engages in co-curricular and extra-curricular activities in collaboration with professional bodies, offering a blend of innovative courses and hands-on projects. Additionally, the department promotes an entrepreneurial spirit, helping students develop skills to innovate and succeed in their own ventures within the construction and engineering sectors.
Vision
To develop competent civil engineers and establish a teaching and learning process that meets global standards for a sustainable environment. We aim to cultivate highly skilled, sustainable diploma civil engineers and entrepreneurs who can address global challenges with the latest technologies and innovations, meeting the evolving needs of the industry and society.
Mission
Provide quality technical knowledge and skills through an updated curriculum, faculty development, and advanced infrastructure.
Equip students with industry-relevant skills by offering training in the latest Civil Engineering technologies and effective communication.
Encourage students to pursue higher education, competitive exams, and career-enhancing courses.
Enable students and faculty to take on leadership roles and contribute to societal betterment in a sustainable manner.
