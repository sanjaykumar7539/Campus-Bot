Admission Procedure
The polytechnic admits both boys and girls. Reservation will be made as per the norms prescribed by the Government of Tamil Nadu. Admission of students under All India Quota and Orthopedically Handicapped are being done under DOTE's special permission.
Age Limit
Candidates must adhere to the age limit set by the Directorate of Technical Education.
Candidates must meet the eligibility conditions such as subjects, marks, and number of attempts as specified by the Directorate of Technical Education, Tamil Nadu.
Category
I Year (3 years duration)
Passed 10th Standard / SSLC Examination
Obtained at least 35% marks in the qualifying examination.
II Year (2 years duration) (Lateral Entry)

or

Duration of Courses
Full-Time Regular Diploma Course: 6 consecutive semesters.

Subjects Of Study
The subjects of study shall include theory and practical as detailed in the Curriculum. The curriculum also includes project work for Full-Time Regular and Sandwich Diploma Courses as well as for Part-Time Regular Diploma Courses and Industrial training for Sandwich Diploma Courses.
