Mechanical Engineering
The Department of Mechanical Engineering, established in 2011, initially admitted 60 students and has since expanded its intake to 120. Over 1,000 graduates have emerged from the department, many of whom hold key positions in private and public organizations in India and abroad, as well as in prestigious educational institutions across the country.
The department features highly qualified faculty and state-of-the-art equipment, with a curriculum that is industry-focused and regularly updated to meet evolving needs. Industry professionals are deeply involved in all aspects of the department and institution. The curriculum emphasizes the latest technological advancements and innovations to prepare students for industrial challenges and entrepreneurial success.
Vision Statement
We aim to produce Mechanical Engineering diploma graduates with strong technical skills and innovative thinking, preparing them for successful careers and entrepreneurship. Our goal is to be globally recognized for excellence in education, developing engineers who excel in addressing industry and societal needs.
Mission
To offer state-of-the-art facilities and high-quality education in Mechanical Engineering, enhancing both intellectual and practical skills.
To systematically enhance students' academic performance, improving their placement potential and readiness for further studies.
To equip students with the confidence to address intellectual and ethical challenges in their careers.
To develop effective technical communication skills in students, both individually and as part of a team.
To foster creative thinking among staff and students, enriching the overall teaching and learning experience.
