E.G.S Pillay Polytechnic College is one of the pioneering Self Financing Polytechnic Colleges in the
State. It was established by the E G S Pillay & Sons Educational & Charitable Trust, Nagapattinam in the year
2011 with the sanction of the Government of Tamilnadu, approval of the All India Council for Technical
Education, New Delhi and Directorate of Technical Education, Tamilnadu. Its courses are affiliated to
DOTE, Chennai.
All Programs For Students
In E.G.S. Pillay Polytechnic College as a Co-Educational Institution, running the following courses.
Basic Engineering
The Basic Engineering program introduces fundamental engineering concepts and principles, laying a solid foundation for advanced studies in various engineering fields.
Know More
Civil Engineering
Our Civil Engineering program equips students with the knowledge to design, construct, and maintain infrastructure such as roads, bridges, and buildings.
Mechanical Engineering
The Mechanical Engineering program focuses on the design, analysis, and manufacturing of mechanical systems, preparing students for careers in various industries.
Electrical & Electronics Engineering
This program covers the principles of electrical systems, electronics, and electromagnetism, providing a comprehensive understanding of electrical engineering applications.
Electronics & Communication Engineering
The Electronics & Communication Engineering program focuses on electronic circuits, communication systems, and signal processing, preparing students for careers in telecommunications and technology.
Computer Engineering
Our Computer Engineering program integrates computer science and electrical engineering, emphasizing hardware and software development for modern computing systems.
