Electrical and Electronics Engineering
The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, established in 2011, prides itself on its strong industry connections. The department boasts state-of-the-art laboratories that support both foundational and advanced courses, including Electrical Machines, Power Electronics, Microprocessors and Microcontrollers, Computer Simulation, and Analog and Digital Electronics.
To ensure students gain comprehensive knowledge and practical skills, the department regularly hosts expert guest lecturers on specialized topics and organizes industrial visits. These initiatives, coupled with opportunities for internships and in-plant training, help students stay aligned with industry trends.
The department’s strength lies in its highly experienced and qualified faculty, who are dedicated to providing an enriching educational experience and preparing students for successful careers in the field.
Vision Statement
The Department is committed to excellence in its academic programs by providing foundational knowledge in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. We aim to prepare students to adapt to global needs and contribute to the development of our country, India.
Mission Statement
To offer high-quality, modern education at the diploma level, consistently enhancing technical knowledge and skills. We are dedicated to upgrading infrastructure and faculty expertise, providing skill-oriented training to meet industrial demands, and fostering the development of students into responsible, capable citizens.
