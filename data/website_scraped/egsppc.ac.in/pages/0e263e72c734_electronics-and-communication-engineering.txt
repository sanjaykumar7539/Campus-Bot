Electronics and Communication Engineering
The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, established in 2011, has quickly become a leader in its field, with a mission to produce outstanding graduates for the electronics and communication industry. Initially starting with an intake of 60 students, the department has built a reputation for its innovative curriculum and projects, ensuring that students stay abreast of the latest advancements in the electronics sector.
The department boasts exceptional infrastructure, including diverse computing platforms, tailored to meet the ever-evolving needs of the electronics industry. Its impact is evident through the high demand for its graduates by employers worldwide.
From its inception, the department has been supported by highly qualified faculty members specializing in applied electronics, electronics and control, and other areas. These faculty members are dedicated to creating a Centre of Excellence in technical education, aiming to develop technocrats with advanced skills and a strong sense of social responsibility.
With a vision to cultivate competent and disciplined electronics and communication engineers, the department continues to set a high standard in technical education.
Vision
To offer quality education in Electronics and Communication Engineering that addresses industry and societal challenges through a need-based curriculum, robust industry-institute interaction, and a student-centric teaching-learning process. We aim to produce diploma graduates who embody innovative and ethical standards.
Mission
To develop high-quality, sustainable engineers through research, innovation, and teamwork, ensuring lasting technological advancements in Electronics and Communication Engineering. The department is committed to creating skilled technical professionals who can meet the dynamic demands of the industry and societal needs.
