Message From Joint-Secretary
E. G. S. Pillay Polytechnic College, Nagapattinam, has been a pivotal platform for the Skill development of thousands of students. Renowned for its high standards in engineering education, the college consistently attracts leading recruiters to its placement drives. This connection fosters a unique bond between students, the college, and industry professionals.
The college holds a special place in my heart, thanks to its dedicated faculty, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and a nurturing academic environment. These attributes distinguish it as a resputed institution in technical education. Our student-centric academic framework promotes holistic development and the cultivation of exceptional professional skills.
Through co-curricular and extra-curricular activities, we nurture versatile Technocrats who make significant contributions to both their professional and social spheres. Emphasizing out-of-the-box thinking and innovative approaches, our goal is to produce world-class engineers who meet the demands of fast-paced industries.
