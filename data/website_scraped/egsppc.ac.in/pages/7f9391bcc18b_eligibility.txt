Eligibility Criteria for Admission
To be eligible for admission to E.G.S. Pillay Polytechnic College in Nagapattinam, you must have passed the 10th grade or equivalent from a recognized board or institute.
General Criteria
General Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for admission, students must meet the following criteria:
Must have completed the 10th grade or equivalent.
Must have passed in the subjects of Mathematics and Science.
Additional Requirements
In addition to the general criteria, applicants must fulfill the following:
Submission of a completed application form.
Provision of relevant documents such as mark sheets and certificates.
Payment of the application fee.
Admission Process
The admission process includes the following steps:
Submit the application form along with required documents.
Attend the admission counseling session.
Complete the enrollment by paying the required fees.
