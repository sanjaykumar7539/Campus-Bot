Anti-Drug Committee
About Anti-Drug Committee
The Anti-Drug Committee is dedicated to achieving a narcotic-free campus by creating awareness, enforcing preventive measures, and coordinating with local authorities to address any drug-related concerns.
Objectives of the Anti-Drug Committee
Our primary objective is to achieve the status of a narcotic-free campus by actively working towards prevention, awareness, and coordination with local authorities.
Responsibilities / Role of the Committee
Awareness:
Create awareness among students about the dangers and consequences of drug abuse.
Prevention:
Ensure that the institution’s premises and surroundings are free from drugs.
Coordination:
Report any information regarding the availability of narcotic drugs to the local police.
