PARTIAL DELIVERY COURSE ON 2303ME030 FAILURE ANALYSIS OF NDT TECHNIQUES INDUSTRIAL RADIOLOGY INSPECTION AND IMAGING
September 29, 2026
10:00 AM
offline Event
The
Department of Mechanical Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, organized a
Partial Delivery Course on “Failure Analysis of NDT Techniques – Industrial Radiology Inspection and Imaging”
for
Final Year Mechanical Engineering students
on
29 and 30 September 2026
.
The sessions were handled by
Mr. T. V. Subash, Senior Manager (Retd.), Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), BHEL – Trichy
. The programme focused on the fundamentals and practical applications of
Non-Destructive Testing, industrial radiography, defect detection, inspection procedures, imaging techniques, and failure analysis
. Through industry-oriented insights and real-world examples, the course aimed to strengthen students’ understanding of modern inspection methods and their importance in ensuring
quality, reliability, and safety in engineering components and industrial systems
