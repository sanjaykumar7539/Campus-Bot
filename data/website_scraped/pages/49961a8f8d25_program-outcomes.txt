Program Outcomes
Program Outcomes (POs)
PO #
Program Outcomes Description
PO1
Engineering Knowledge:
Apply knowledge of mathematics, linear algebra, probability, statistics, computing fundamentals, and artificial intelligence specialization to solve complex engineering and data-driven problems.
PO2
Problem Analysis:
Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex AI & ML problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, statistics, and machine learning sciences.
PO3
Design/Development of Solutions:
Design intelligent solutions, predictive models, and deep learning architectures that meet specified needs with appropriate consideration for public health, societal welfare, safety, and cultural considerations.
PO4
Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems:
Use research-based methods including exploratory data analysis, neural network hyperparameter tuning, model validation, and statistical synthesis to generate valid scientific insights.
PO5
Modern Tool Usage:
Create, select, and apply state-of-the-art AI frameworks, GPU compute clusters, and machine learning platforms (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-Learn, OpenCV, Hugging Face) to complex engineering activities.
PO6
The Engineer and Society:
Apply informed reasoning to assess societal, legal, privacy, fairness, and ethical issues relevant to deploying AI systems in healthcare, governance, finance, and automation.
PO7
Environment and Sustainability:
Understand the environmental impact of compute-intensive AI architectures and demonstrate the knowledge of energy-efficient, sustainable Green AI practices.
PO8
Ethics:
Apply ethical principles and commit to responsible AI development, algorithmic transparency, bias mitigation, and professional norms of engineering practice.
PO9
Individual and Team Work:
Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse cross-functional teams in multidisciplinary data science settings.
PO10
Communication:
Communicate effectively on complex AI concepts, model behaviors, and quantitative findings with both technical researchers and domain stakeholders.
PO11
Project Management and Finance:
Demonstrate understanding of AI lifecycle management, MLOps deployment pipelines, and apply engineering economics to manage AI development projects.
PO12
Life-long Learning:
Recognize the fast-paced evolution of artificial intelligence and possess the capability to engage in independent and lifelong learning across cutting-edge frontiers of AI research.
