WEBINAR ON GENERATIVE AI: SKILLS FOR THE FUTURE
August 21, 2026
07:00 AM
online Event
webinar is an academic and career-oriented event organized by the
Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, especially for
second-year AI & Data Science students
. The session aims to introduce students to the growing field of Generative AI and help them understand how AI-powered technologies are transforming education, software development, and the modern workplace.The webinar will feature
Mr. Guhan Masilamani, Jr. Software Engineer at Cognizant, Chennai
, as the resource person. Through his industry experience and insights, students will gain a practical understanding of emerging Generative AI concepts, relevant skills, potential applications, and the importance of staying updated with rapidly evolving AI technologies.
