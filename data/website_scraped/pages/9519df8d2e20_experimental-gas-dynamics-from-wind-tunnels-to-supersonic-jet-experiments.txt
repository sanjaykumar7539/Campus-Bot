Experimental Gas Dynamics - From wind tunnels to supersonic jet experiments
August 17, 2026
08:00 AM
online Event
The webinar provides a comprehensive introduction to the
fundamentals and practical aspects of Experimental Gas Dynamics
, with a focus on understanding the behavior of compressible flows through laboratory-based experiments and advanced testing techniques. The session will introduce participants to the principles and applications of
wind-tunnel testing
, including the design, operation, and significance of different types of wind tunnels used for aerodynamic investigations. The webinar will also cover various
flow measurement and instrumentation techniques
used to determine important parameters such as pressure, temperature, velocity, Mach number, and density in compressible-flow environments. Participants will gain an understanding of
compressible flow testing
, shock waves, expansion waves, and other important flow phenomena encountered at high speeds.
