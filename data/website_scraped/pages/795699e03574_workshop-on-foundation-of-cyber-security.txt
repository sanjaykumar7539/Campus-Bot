WORKSHOP ON FOUNDATION OF CYBER SECURITY
October 05, 2026
09:30 AM
offline Event
The
Department of Cyber Security, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, is organizing a
Workshop on “Foundation of Cyber Security”
for
First-Year Cyber Security students
on
5 October 2026
.
The session will be handled by
Mr. Kappala Roshan, SME – Cyber Security, HCLTech, Chennai
. The workshop is designed to introduce students to the fundamental concepts of cybersecurity, including cyber threats, security principles, safe digital practices, and the importance of protecting systems and data in today’s connected world. Through industry-oriented insights and practical perspectives, the programme aims to build a strong foundation in cybersecurity and encourage students to develop the technical awareness required for future learning and professional growth.
