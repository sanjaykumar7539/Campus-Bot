AI Frontier: Discovering the Intelligence & Machine Learning
is a one-day workshop organized by the
Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML), E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, exclusively for
first-year AIML students
.
The workshop is designed to introduce students to the exciting and rapidly evolving fields of
Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
. It will provide participants with a foundational understanding of AI concepts, machine learning techniques, real-world applications, and emerging technologies. The session aims to encourage students to explore the possibilities of intelligent systems and develop an interest in pursuing further learning and innovation in AI and ML.
