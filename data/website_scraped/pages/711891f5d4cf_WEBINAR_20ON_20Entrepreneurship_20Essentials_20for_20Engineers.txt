WEBINAR ON Entrepreneurship Essentials for Engineers
September 26, 2026
10:00 AM
online Event
The
Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam
, organized a webinar on
“Entrepreneurship Essentials for Engineers”
on
26 September 2026
.
The programme was aimed at creating awareness among ECE students about the fundamentals of
entrepreneurship, innovation, startup development, and career opportunities beyond conventional employment
. The session highlighted how engineering knowledge can be transformed into practical solutions, business ideas, and technology-driven ventures.
The webinar was handled by
Mr. J. Vijayan, Associate AI and Data Instructor, Maanavan Upskills
, who shared valuable insights on the entrepreneurial mindset required for engineers in today’s rapidly evolving technology landscape. The session focused on helping students understand how to identify real-world problems, develop innovative ideas, evaluate opportunities, and convert technical concepts into viable products or services.
