Emerging Trends in Industrial Robotics and Automation
is a Professional Society Event – III Webinar organized by the
Department of Mechanical Engineering, EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
.
The webinar focuses on the latest developments, emerging technologies, and future opportunities in
industrial robotics and automation
. It aims to provide students with valuable insights into how robotics and automation are transforming modern manufacturing industries, improving productivity, precision, safety, and efficiency.
The session will be led by
Mr. D. Sundar, CEO, Suntek Automation & Solution, Kumbakonam
, who will share his industry experience and practical knowledge of robotics and automation.
