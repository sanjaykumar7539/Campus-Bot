SEMINAR ON SUSTAINABLES DEVELOPMENT GOALS (SDG) CONNECT 2030 STUDENTS AWARENESS PROGRAMME
August 19, 2026
02:00 AM
offline Event
The programme aims to create awareness among students about the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and their importance in achieving a sustainable and inclusive future by 2030. The session will introduce students to the key SDGs, their relevance to environmental protection, social development, climate action, and responsible resource utilization, with a focus on practical actions that can be adopted in everyday life and academic activities.
