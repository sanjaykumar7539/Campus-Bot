06 DAYS ONLINE SHORT-TERM TRAINING PROGRAM ON "LAND USE LAND COVER (LULC) MAPPING AND CLASSIFICATION USING GIS AND REMOTE SENSING TECHNIQUES"
July 13, 2026
06:00 AM
online Event
Land Use Land Cover (LULC) mapping plays a vital role in environmental :
monitoring, urban planning, agriculture, water resources management, disaster mitigation and sustainable development. Recent advancements in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, and cloud- based geospatial technologies have significantly improved the accuracy and efficiency of LULC classification and change detection studies. This Six-Day Online STTP is designed to provide participants with both theoretical knowledge and practical exposure to modern GIS and Remote Sensing techniques used for LULC mapping and classification. The program includes hands-on training using satellite datasets, image processing techniques, classification algorithms, accuracy.
