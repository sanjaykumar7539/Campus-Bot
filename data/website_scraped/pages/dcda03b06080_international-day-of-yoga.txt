Aligned with this year’s theme, "Yoga for Healthy Ageing," our students and faculty came together to promote the importance of yoga in achieving physical vitality, mental well-being, and a balanced lifestyle. Through guided yoga sessions and awareness activities, participants experienced the transformative benefits of yoga for lifelong health and wellness.Yoga is more than exercise—it is a journey toward inner harmony, mindfulness, and overall well-being. Through regular practice, we cultivate strength, resilience, focus, and positivity in our daily lives.
