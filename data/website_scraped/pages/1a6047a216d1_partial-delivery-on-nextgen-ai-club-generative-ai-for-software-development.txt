offline Event
NextGen AI Club – Generative AI for Software Development
is a two-day technical program organized by the
Department of Master of Computer Applications (MCA), EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, as part of the NextGen AI Club initiative.
The event is designed for
2nd-year MCA students
and focuses on the practical application of
Generative AI in modern software development
. Participants will gain exposure to emerging AI tools, techniques, and workflows that can enhance software development, improve productivity, assist in coding, and support innovative problem-solving.
The program aims to bridge the gap between
academic learning and industry practices
by helping students understand how Generative AI is transforming the software development lifecycle and preparing them for the evolving demands of the technology industry.
