Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO #
Programme Educational Objectives Description
PEO1
Core Expertise & Algorithmic Excellence:
Graduates will excel in applying mathematical foundations, statistical learning models, and deep learning algorithms to solve complex data-driven and autonomous challenges.
PEO2
Professional Leadership & AI Innovation:
Graduates will achieve successful careers as AI Engineers, Data Scientists, Machine Learning Researchers, and Entrepreneurs, contributing innovative technologies to global industries.
PEO3
Ethics, Social Impact & Lifelong Growth:
Graduates will champion ethical AI principles, promote transparency and fairness in automated systems, and actively engage in lifelong learning and interdisciplinary research.
