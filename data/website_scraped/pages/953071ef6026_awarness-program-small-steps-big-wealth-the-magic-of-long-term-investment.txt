Expanded Event Description
The
Department of Master of Computer Applications, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam
, is organizing an
Awareness Program
titled
“Small Steps, Big Wealth: The Magic of Long-Term Investment”
for the students of
I & II MCA
.
The program is designed to create awareness among students about the importance of
financial planning, disciplined saving, and long-term investment strategies
. It aims to help participants understand how small and consistent financial decisions made at an early stage can contribute significantly to building wealth over time. The session will also highlight the concept of
compounding, investment discipline, financial goal setting, risk awareness, and the importance of starting investments early
