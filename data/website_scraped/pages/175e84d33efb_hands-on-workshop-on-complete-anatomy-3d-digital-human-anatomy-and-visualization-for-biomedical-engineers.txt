HANDS-ON WORKSHOP ON COMPLETE ANATOMY: 3D DIGITAL HUMAN ANATOMY AND VISUALIZATION FOR BIOMEDICAL ENGINEERS
September 01, 2026
10:00 AM
offline Event
The
Department of Biomedical Engineering & IQAC, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous)
is organizing a
Hands-on Workshop on “Complete Anatomy: 3D Digital Human Anatomy and Visualization for Biomedical Engineers”
for
2nd Year Biomedical Engineering students
.
The workshop aims to provide students with practical exposure to
3D digital human anatomy, anatomical visualization, and modern digital tools
used in biomedical engineering education and applications. The session will help participants develop a better understanding of human anatomy through interactive and immersive visualization techniques, bridging theoretical learning with practical applications.
The workshop will be conducted by
Mr. A. Robert Sahayaraj, Sales Consultant, Elsevier, Gurgaon
, who will demonstrate the features and applications of
Complete Anatomy
, enabling students to explore anatomical structures in an engaging and technology-driven manner
