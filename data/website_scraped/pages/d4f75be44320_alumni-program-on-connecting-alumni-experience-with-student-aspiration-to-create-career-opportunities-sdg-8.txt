ALUMNI PROGRAM ON CONNECTING ALUMNI EXPERIENCE WITH STUDENT ASPIRATION TO CREATE CAREER OPPORTUNITIES - (SDG 8)
August 13, 2026
02:00 AM
offline Event
The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering organized an
Alumni Program on “Connecting Alumni Experience with Student Aspiration to Create Career Opportunities – SDG 8”
for III Year ECE students. The program aimed to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations by providing students with valuable insights from an experienced alumnus. The interaction encouraged students to understand current industry trends, explore career pathways, set professional goals, and make informed decisions about their future. The program also highlighted the importance of skill development, entrepreneurship, adaptability, and continuous learning in achieving sustainable employment and career growth, aligning with
Sustainable Development Goal 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth)
.
