HIGHER STUDIES PROGRAM Awareness for Higher studies and GATE Examination
October 05, 2026
02:30 AM
offline Event
The
Department of Civil Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam
, is organizing a
Higher Studies Program
titled
“Awareness for Higher Studies and GATE Examination”
for the benefit of
Third Year and Final Year Civil Engineering students
.
The program is intended to create a clear understanding among students about the importance of pursuing
higher education and preparing for the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE)
. The session will provide guidance on the opportunities available through GATE, including admission to reputed institutions for postgraduate studies, pathways toward advanced technical education, and the role of competitive examinations in strengthening academic and professional prospects.
