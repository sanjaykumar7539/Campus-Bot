ALUMNI WEBINAR ON VARIOUS OPPORTUNITIES IN CIVIL ENGINEERING SECTOR
August 21, 2026
02:30 AM
online Event
The webinar aims to provide
2nd, 3rd, and final-year Civil Engineering students
with valuable insights into the diverse career opportunities available in the civil engineering industry. The session will be led by
Mr. M. Sudhahar
, a Quantity Surveyor – Commercial at
Sobha Modular IND L.L.C, UAE
, who will share his professional experience, industry knowledge, and guidance on career pathways in the field. The webinar will help students understand
industry expectations, career prospects, quantity surveying, international opportunities, and the skills required to build a successful career in the civil engineering sector
. It is a valuable opportunity for students to interact with an experienced alumnus and gain practical insights into the professional world.
