offline Event
The Department of Computer Science & Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam, organized a
Partial Delivery Session on “Web-Based Projects and Its Impacts”
for the
III Year CSE ‘B’ students
on
1 October 2026
.
The session was delivered by
Mr. Guhan Masilamani, Junior Software Engineer, Cognizant, Chennai
, who shared practical insights into web-based project development, real-world implementation, and the impact of modern web technologies in the software industry. The programme aimed to strengthen students’ technical understanding, enhance their project-development skills, and provide valuable industry exposure through practical knowledge and professional experience.
