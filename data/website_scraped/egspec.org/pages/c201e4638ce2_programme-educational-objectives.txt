Programme Educational Objectives (PEO)
##
Programme Educational Objectives
PEO1
The Graduates will have an integrated knowledge, analytical and professional skills to perform as management professionals and team player to handle the business issues for the benefits of the society using latest technologies.
PEO2
The Graduates will have positive perspectives and communicative skills that meet the needs of Indian and Global standards with professional ethics and values.
PEO3
The Graduates will involve themselves in life-long learning and research activities and take up leadership role through innovative strategies and to become successful entrepreneurs.
