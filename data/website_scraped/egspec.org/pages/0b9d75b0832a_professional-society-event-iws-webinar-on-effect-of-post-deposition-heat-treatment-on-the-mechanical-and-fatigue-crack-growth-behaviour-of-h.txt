"Professional Society Event-IWS WEBINAR ON Effect of Post-Deposition Heat Treatment on the Mechanical and Fatigue Crack Growth Behaviour of Hot-Wire TIG Wire Arc Additively Manufactured Ni Alloy 740H"
September 28, 2026
06:30 AM
online Event
The
Department of Mechanical Engineering, EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, is organizing a
Professional Society Event – IWS Webinar
on the topic
“Effect of Post-Deposition Heat Treatment on the Mechanical and Fatigue Crack Growth Behaviour of Hot-Wire TIG Wire Arc Additively Manufactured Ni Alloy 740H.”
The webinar will provide Mechanical Engineering students with valuable insights into
wire arc additive manufacturing, Hot-Wire TIG technology, post-deposition heat treatment, mechanical properties, and fatigue crack growth behaviour of Ni Alloy 740H
. The session will help students understand recent developments in advanced manufacturing and welding technologies and their applications in engineering.
