ALUMNI INTERACTION ARCHITECTS OF WEALTH: WHERE FRESH MANAGEMENT TALENT MEETS PRIVATE BANKING
September 09, 2026
11:30 AM
offline Event
The
Department of Management Studies, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College
, organizes an
Alumni Interaction
titled
“Architects of Wealth: Where Fresh Management Talent Meets Private Banking.”
The session is designed to provide
I MBA students
with valuable insights into the private banking sector, relationship management, wealth creation, and career opportunities in the banking and financial services industry.
The interaction will feature
Mr. Balaji Paramasivam
, an alumnus of the
2022–2024 batch
and currently serving as
Senior Associate – Relationship Manager at City Union Bank, Porayar
. Through his professional experience and industry perspective, students will gain practical knowledge about private banking operations, client relationship management, financial services, and the skills required to build a successful career in banking.
The session aims to
bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practices
, while encouraging students to interact with an experienced alumnus, understand real-world career pathways, and develop professional competencies relevant to the banking and management sectors.
