MOTIVATIONAL TALK ON DREAM BIG ACHIEVE BIG
September 12, 2026
09:30 AM
offline Event
The Department of Science and Humanities at E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam, organized a full-day motivational talk titled
"DREAM BIG ACHIEVE BIG"
aimed at inspiring first-year students to set ambitious goals and build a strong foundation for their academic and personal journeys.
Core Message:
Emphasizing that "Success is Dependent on Effort," the session focused on developing essential life skills, personal development, and mindset building.
Mindset & Goal Setting:
Inspiring students to think beyond traditional boundaries, set ambitious personal and academic milestones, and adopt a growth-oriented mindset.
Effort & Perseverance:
Centered on the core theme
"Success is Dependent on Effort,"
the talk emphasized consistent discipline, hard work, and dynamic problem-solving as essential pillars for bridging the gap between dreaming and achieving.
Essential Life Skills:
Practical techniques were shared on handling academic pressure, building emotional intelligence, time management, and adapting effectively to college life.
