Seminar on Civil Engineering 2.0: Building the Future with AI
The Department of Civil Engineering and the Hub Civilax Club of EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam, are organizing an online seminar titled
“Civil Engineering 2.0: Building the Future with AI”
on 11th October 2026, from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM.
The seminar will feature
Dr. B. Vinodhkumar, Professor at V.S.B. Engineering College, Karur
, as the resource person. The session aims to introduce students to the growing role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in civil engineering and explore how emerging technologies are transforming the construction industry, structural design, project planning, and infrastructure development.
