TECHNICAL EVENT MATHTECH-INAUGURAL
October 14, 2026
11:00 AM
offline Event
The Department of Science and Humanities at EGS Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam, is organizing
“MATHTECH – Inaugural,”
a technical event designed for all first-year students on
14th October 2026
.The event aims to highlight the importance of mathematics in engineering and technology while encouraging students to develop logical thinking, analytical skills, and problem-solving abilities. It serves as an introductory platform to foster students’ interest in mathematics and explore its practical applications in various engineering disciplines.The programme will feature
Mr. K. Satiskumar
, B.Tech–EEE, from SASTRA Deemed to be University, Thanjavur, as the resource person. His participation will provide students with valuable insights into the role of mathematical concepts in engineering education and technical applications.The event is convened by
Dr. R. Deepa, Head/Professor, Department of Science and Humanities
, and coordinated by
Ms. R. Rajeshwari, Math Trainer, Department of Science and Humanities
.The programme will be held on
14th October 2026, from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM, at SJB-201
. This initiative aims to create an engaging learning environment, strengthen mathematical understanding, and inspire first-year students to appreciate the connection between mathematics, innovation, and engineering.
