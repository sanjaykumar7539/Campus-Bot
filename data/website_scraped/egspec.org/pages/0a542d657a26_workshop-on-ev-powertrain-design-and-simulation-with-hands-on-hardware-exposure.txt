WORKSHOP ON EV POWERTRAIN DESIGN AND SIMULATION WITH HANDS-ON HARDWARE EXPOSURE
September 24, 2026
09:15 AM
offline Event
The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, organizes a
two-day workshop on “EV Powertrain Design and Simulation with Hands-on Hardware Exposure”
exclusively for third-year EEE students. The workshop aims to provide participants with practical knowledge of
electric vehicle (EV) powertrain architecture, design principles, simulation techniques, and hardware implementation
.
The sessions will introduce students to the major components and operating principles of EV powertrains, including
electric motors, power converters, battery systems, motor control, and power management
. Participants will also gain exposure to simulation-based design and understand how theoretical concepts are applied in real-world electric vehicle systems.
