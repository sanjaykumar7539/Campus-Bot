Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
##
Program Educational Objectives
PEO1
To develop knowledge and competency for successful careers in information technology.
PEO2
Ability to design and implement software systems to solve real life problems in the society with an understanding of professional ethical standards.
PEO3
Motivate the students to pursue higher studies in engineering or other professional fields.
PEO4
To train the graduates to communicate effectively as individuals and as team members in multidisciplinary projects, research and development activities
