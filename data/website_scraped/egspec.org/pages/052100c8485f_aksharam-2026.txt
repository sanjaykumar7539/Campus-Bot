AKSHARAM
is the annual cultural festival of E.G.S. Pillay Group of Institutions, Nagapattinam. It stands as a beacon of creativity, bringing together the brightest talents from across Tamil Nadu for an unforgettable celebration of music, culture, and talent.
From electrifying celebrity performances to cut-throat cultural competitions, AKSHARAM offers a platform for students to express themselves, innovate, and connect. Experience the pulse of the youth in a festival that blends tradition with modern flair.
