GATE 2027 – Awareness, Strategy & Success for Mechanical Engineering Students”
is a career guidance and awareness programme organized by the
Career Guidance Cell (CGC), EGSPEC
, under the
Talent Talks 2026–27
initiative. The session is designed specifically for
third-year Mechanical Engineering students
to provide comprehensive insights into the
GATE 2027 examination
, its significance, preparation strategies, and career opportunities.
The programme will focus on understanding the GATE examination pattern, identifying key subject areas, developing effective preparation strategies, managing time efficiently, and setting realistic goals. The session will also highlight the various
higher education and career opportunities
available through GATE, motivating students to plan their preparation at an early stage.
