Department Highlights
S.No
Salient Features & Key Highlights
1
Advanced Cyber Defense & Digital Forensics Lab:
High-performance workstations equipped with state-of-the-art security suites, vulnerability scanners (Kali Linux, Wireshark, Metasploit, Nessus, Autopsy, and Ghidra).
2
Industry Certifications Integration:
Structured curriculum enabling students to earn prestigious certifications like CompTIA Security+, CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker), and Cisco CyberOps Associate.
3
Capture The Flag (CTF) & Ethical Hacking Club:
Active student community participating in national and international cybersecurity hackathons, vulnerability research, and bug bounty initiatives.
4
Industry MoUs & Collaboration:
Partnerships with top cybersecurity firms and IT solution providers for live project mentorship, internships, and industrial threat intelligence exposure.
5
Security Operations Center (SOC) Simulation:
Hands-on training on SIEM tools, network anomaly detection, intrusion prevention, and live incident response workflows.
6
Research & Innovation in Secure Computing:
Focus areas include AI-driven threat mitigation, blockchain security, IoT & SCADA protection, and zero-trust network architectures.
7
100% Placement & Internship Support:
Dedicated career pathways into roles such as Cyber Security Analyst, Penetration Tester, Forensics Examiner, and Cloud Security Engineer.
