WORKSHOP ON OUTCOME-BASED EDUCATION (OBE): COURSE OUTCOMES, CO-PO MAPPING, ATTAINMENT AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
August 26, 2026
10:00 AM
online Event
The workshop is designed to enhance faculty members’ understanding of
Outcome-Based Education and its effective implementation in higher education
. The session will focus on developing and assessing Course Outcomes (COs), understanding
CO–PO mapping
, measuring outcome attainment, and using assessment results to promote
continuous improvement in teaching and learning practices
. The workshop will be conducted by
Dr. S. Baskar
, Professor, Department of EEE and Dean (R&D), Thiagarajar College of Engineering, Madurai. His academic and institutional experience will provide participants with valuable insights into effective OBE practices and outcome assessment methodologies.
