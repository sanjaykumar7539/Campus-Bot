GUEST LECTURE ON WHY HRM IS IMPORTANT FOR COMPUTER APPLICATIONS
September 23, 2026
11:30 AM
offline Event
The Department of Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam, is hosting an insightful guest lecture titled
"Why HRM is Important for Computer Applications."
Designed specifically for technology students and future software professionals, this session bridges the gap between technical system development and human resource management (HRM) concepts. Understanding organizational structure, payroll workflows, talent acquisition, and employee management systems is essential for software engineers building modern enterprise resource planning (ERP) solutions, HR technology software, and workplace management tools.
