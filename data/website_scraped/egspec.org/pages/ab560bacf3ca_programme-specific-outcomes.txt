Programme Specific Outcomes Description
PSO1
AI/ML Design & Algorithmic Modelling:
Ability to design, formulate, train, and evaluate predictive machine learning models, deep neural networks, computer vision algorithms, and natural language pipelines to address real-world industrial and societal problems.
PSO2
Modern AI Tool Stack & MLOps Deployment:
Proficiency in industry-standard AI platforms (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-Learn, OpenCV, Hugging Face, ROS), GPU acceleration techniques, and cloud-based MLOps workflows to deploy scalable, robust intelligent solutions.
