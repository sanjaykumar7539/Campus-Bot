Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO #
Programme Educational Objectives Description
PEO1
Graduates will establish successful careers as cybersecurity professionals, security analysts, consultants, researchers, or entrepreneurs in government, industry, and academia.
PEO2
Graduates will apply cybersecurity principles, emerging technologies, and analytical skills to design, implement, and manage secure computing systems that address evolving cyber threats.
PEO3
Graduates will pursue higher education, professional certifications, research, lifelong learning, and demonstrate ethical, legal, and social responsibility in professional practice.
