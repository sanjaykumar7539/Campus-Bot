AI TOOL ORIENTATION FACULTY ORIENTATION PROGRAM ON AICTE-PGQID
September 29, 2026
02:00 AM
offline Event
The
Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam
, organizes an
AI Tool Orientation – Faculty Orientation Program on AICTE-PGQID
on
29 September 2026
.
The programme is designed to create greater awareness among faculty members about the growing role of
Artificial Intelligence tools in teaching, learning, research, academic administration, and professional development
. The session will introduce participants to the effective and responsible use of AI-enabled tools that can support academic activities, improve productivity, simplify routine tasks, and encourage innovative approaches in higher education.
The orientation will be handled by
Dr. T. Suresh Padmanabhan, Professor, Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College
. Drawing on his academic experience, the resource person will provide practical insights into how AI tools can be integrated into day-to-day faculty activities, including
content preparation, lesson planning, research assistance, data analysis, documentation, presentation development, assessment support, and knowledge enhancement
