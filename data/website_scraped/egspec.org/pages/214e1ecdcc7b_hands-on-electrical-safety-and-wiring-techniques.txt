HANDS-ON ELECTRICAL SAFETY AND WIRING TECHNIQUES
August 27, 2026
09:20 AM
offline Event
The workshop focused on providing students with practical knowledge of electrical safety practices, wiring techniques, electrical components, and safe handling of electrical systems. Through hands-on demonstrations and practical activities, students gained a better understanding of electrical wiring, safety precautions, fault identification, and the correct procedures for working with electrical installations.
The programme aimed to bridge the gap between theoretical learning and practical application while developing students’ technical skills, safety awareness, and confidence in handling basic electrical systems. The session provided valuable industrial exposure and enhanced the students’ understanding of safe and efficient electrical practices.
