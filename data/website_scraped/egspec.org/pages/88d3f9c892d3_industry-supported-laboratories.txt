Industry Supported Laboratories
Why Industry-Supported Laboratories Are Important
1. Bridge the Gap Between Academia and Industry:
Industry-supported labs ensure that students are exposed to real-world tools, technologies, and workflows, reducing the mismatch between academic learning and industry expectations.
2. Access to Latest Technologies:
These labs are equipped with cutting-edge hardware, software, and platforms provided by industry partners. This keeps students updated with the latest trends, which traditional academic labs may not always offer.
3. Industry-Recognized Certifications:
Many companies offer training and certification programs through these labs, allowing students to earn globally recognized credentials that improve career prospects.
4. Internship and Placement Opportunities:
Collaboration with industry improves chances for Internships, Industrial projects, and Direct recruitment. Companies often consider students trained in their supported labs as pre-skilled candidates.
5. Exposure to Real Industry Projects:
Students and faculty can work on live projects, case studies, and product development activities directly supported by industry experts.
6. Faculty Development and Up-skilling:
Faculty members receive training and workshops from industry specialists, which keeps teaching in line with current industry standards.
7. Research and Innovation Support:
Industry labs often fund or guide Research initiatives, Student startups, and Innovation challenges and hackathons. This boosts the institution’s research output and visibility.
8. Improved Institutional Reputation:
Having industry-supported labs positions the institution as Industry-aligned, Progressive, and Technology-ready. It enhances the credibility of academic programs and attracts better admissions.
9. Supports Outcome-Based Education (OBE):
These labs directly contribute to achieving Program Outcomes (POs) and Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs) by providing measurable hands-on learning experiences.

Oracle Workforce Development Program

To create physical relational database tables to implement a database design

To create, maintain, and manipulate database objects
To prepare for Oracle 10g - SQL and JAVA Programming
Networking (CISCO) Laboratory
Learn the basic concepts of computer networking and acquire practical notions of protocols with the emphasis on TCP/IP.
To receive the internship from networking companies
To understand the simulation concepts of wireless and wired networks.
Autodesk Design Lab
Develop Industry-Ready Design Skills:
To equip students with practical skills in 3D modeling, drafting, simulation, and visualization using Autodesk tools such as AutoCAD, Fusion 360, Revit, Maya, and Inventor.
Promote Industry-Standard Design Practices:
To familiarize learners with international design standards, workflows, and best practices widely used in engineering, architecture, and manufacturing industries.
Enable Hands-on Learning Through Advanced Tools:
To provide students with hands-on exposure to Autodesk’s professional design and engineering software, enabling them to create accurate technical drawings, models, and prototypes.
Enhance Innovation and Creativity:
To encourage innovative thinking by allowing students to conceptualize, design, and simulate products or structures in a virtual environment.
Altair Centre of Excellence
Develop Advanced Simulation and Engineering Analysis Skills:
To equip students with hands-on expertise in CAE, FEA, CFD, MBD, and optimization using Altair’s industry-leading tools.
Promote Industry-Standard Engineering Practices:
To train learners in industry-validated simulation workflows, ensuring accuracy and reliability.
Enable End-to-End Product Development:
To help students understand the full digital engineering lifecycle using Altair’s integrated platform.
Foster Data-Driven Engineering:
To introduce students to AI/ML-enabled design exploration and predictive modeling.
Enhance Multidisciplinary Engineering Problem Solving:
To enable students to analyze complex systems identifying structures, fluids, thermal behavior, etc.
Improve Productivity Through High-Performance Computing:
To train students on Altair PBS Works and cloud-based simulation.
VMware IT Academy
Develop Core Virtualization Skills:
To equip students with practical skills in server, desktop, and network virtualization using VMware tools.
Enable Cloud Computing Competency:
To introduce learners to cloud architectures and management using VMware Cloud Foundation.
Promote Data Center Modernization Knowledge:
To help students understand modern IT infrastructures including SDDC and virtualized storage.
Build High Availability and Disaster Recovery Expertise:
To train students in configuring HA clusters, Fault Tolerance, and Disaster Recovery.
Strengthen Skills in Network Virtualization:
To educate students on VMware NSX and virtual network management.
TRIONE CADD
Develop Advanced CAD Modeling Competency:
To train students in creating complex 3D models using CATIA and CREO.
Strengthen Product Design and Development Skills:
To enable learners to understand the complete product lifecycle from design to manufacturing.
Enhance Surface and Solid Modeling Expertise:
To build strong capabilities in Solid modeling, Surface modeling, Sheet metal design, and Assembly design.

IoT & Embedded Systems Lab (Enthu Tech)

To offer structured modules aligned with academic syllabi (Data structures, Algorithms, OOP, Database connectivity).
