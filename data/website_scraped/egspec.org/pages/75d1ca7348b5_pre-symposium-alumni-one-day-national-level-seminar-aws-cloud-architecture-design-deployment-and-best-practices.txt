AWS Cloud Architecture: Design, Deployment and Best Practices
is a
Pre-Symposium Alumni One-Day National Level Seminar
organized by the
Department of Master of Computer Applications (MCA), EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
.
The seminar is designed for
1st-year MCA students
and aims to provide them with a strong foundation in
cloud computing and AWS cloud architecture
. The session will cover key concepts related to designing and deploying cloud-based solutions, AWS architecture principles, and industry best practices for building secure, scalable, and efficient cloud applications.
The resource person,
Mr. Ravidhara(n), Associate Consultant at CGI Technologies, Chennai, and an alumnus of EGS Pillay Engineering College (Batch 2003–2006)
, will share valuable industry insights and practical knowledge gained from his professional experience in the technology sector.
