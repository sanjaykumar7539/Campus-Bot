Career Pathways in SAP : Industry Insights, Skills & Opportunities
August 12, 2026
09:30 AM
offline Event
Talent Talks – “Career Pathways in SAP: Industry Insights, Skills & Opportunities” is a career-oriented awareness session organized by the Career Guidance Cell, EGS Pillay Engineering College, for Mechanical Engineering students. The session aims to provide students with practical insights into SAP careers, industry requirements, essential technical and professional skills, and emerging career opportunities.
The session will be delivered by Mr. B. Sivaganesh, Team Lead – SAP Functional Architect at Capgemini, Bangalore, who will share his industry experience and guidance on building a successful career in SAP. The interaction will help students understand the relevance of SAP in modern industries, identify the skills expected by employers, and explore suitable career pathways.
