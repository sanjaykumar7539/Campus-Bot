Programme Specific Outcomes
Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
PSO
Description
PSO-1
Demonstrate proficiency by applying concepts, and methodologies of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science and its subdomains to solve engineering, real-world, social or industry problems.
PSO-2
Ability to choose, select & identify relevant Artificial Intelligence & Data Science tools to develop solutions for engineering or industry problems.
PSO-3
Ability to develop software-driven solutions and design system components or processes that meet the specified requirements for solving complex engineering problems or industry problems.
