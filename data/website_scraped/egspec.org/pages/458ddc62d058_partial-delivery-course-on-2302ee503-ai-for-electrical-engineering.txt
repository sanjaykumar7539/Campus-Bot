PARTIAL DELIVERY COURSE ON 2302EE503 AI FOR ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING
October 13, 2026
09:30 AM
offline Event
The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at EGS Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam, is organizing a
Partial Delivery Course on “2302EE503 – AI for Electrical Engineering”
for III-year EEE students on
12th and 13th October 2026
.
The programme aims to introduce students to the fundamentals and practical applications of Artificial Intelligence in Electrical Engineering. The course will help students understand how AI technologies can be applied to electrical systems, automation, robotics, intelligent control, and emerging engineering applications.
The session will feature
Mr. S. Pratheep
, Senior Robotics Engineer at Robomatic – A Division of Cadd Technologies, Coimbatore, as the resource person. His industry experience will provide students with valuable insights into real-world applications of AI and robotics.
The programme is convened by
Dr. Suresh Babu P J, ASP & HoD/EEE
, with coordination by
Dr. T. Suresh Padmanabhan, AP/EEE
, and
Dr. Vinothkumar M, Prof/EEE
The course will be conducted at the
EEE Simulation Laboratory
from
9:30 AM onwards
. This initiative aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and industrial practices while enhancing students’ technical knowledge, practical skills, and understanding of AI-driven innovations in Electrical Engineering.
