INDUSTRY INTERACTION PROGRAM FROM CLASSROOM TO CODEBASE:BUILDING YOUR SOFTWARE CAREER
August 28, 2026
09:30 AM
offline Event
The interaction covered important aspects of software careers, including developing programming skills, working on real-world projects, understanding industry expectations, building a strong technical profile, and preparing for software engineering opportunities. The resource person shared practical industry insights and career guidance, enabling students to connect their classroom knowledge with real-world software development practices. The program aimed to enhance students’ technical awareness, career readiness, problem-solving abilities, and understanding of current industry requirements. It provided students with valuable exposure to the professional software environment and motivated them to develop the skills needed for a successful career in the technology sector.
