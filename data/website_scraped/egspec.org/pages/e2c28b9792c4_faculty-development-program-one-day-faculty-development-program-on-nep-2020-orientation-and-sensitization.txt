FACULTY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM ONE-DAY FACULTY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM ON NEP 2020: ORIENTATION AND SENSITIZATION
August 27, 2026
10:00 AM
offline Event
The Department of Master of Computer Applications (MCA), EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam, organized a One-Day Faculty Development Programme (FDP) on “NEP 2020: Orientation and Sensitization” on 27 August 2026 at the MCA Lab. The programme was conducted to create awareness and provide a comprehensive understanding of the key provisions, objectives, and implementation strategies of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 among the faculty members. The session was handled by Dr. G. Kumaravelan, Associate Professor and Head, School of Engineering and Technology, Department of Computer Science, Pondicherry University, Karaikal Campus, as the Resource Person. The programme focused on sensitizing faculty members to the transformative changes proposed under NEP 2020 and encouraging them to adopt learner-centric, multidisciplinary, skill-oriented, and innovative approaches in higher education.
