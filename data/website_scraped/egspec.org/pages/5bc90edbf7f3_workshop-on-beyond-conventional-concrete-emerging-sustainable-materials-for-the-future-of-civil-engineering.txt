WORKSHOP ON BEYOND CONVENTIONAL CONCRETE: EMERGING SUSTAINABLE MATERIALS FOR THE FUTURE OF CIVIL ENGINEERING
September 09, 2026
10:00 AM
offline Event
The
Department of Civil Engineering
, in association with the
Indian Concrete Institute (Trichy Centre)
, organizes a workshop titled
“Beyond Conventional Concrete: Emerging Sustainable Materials for the Future of Civil Engineering.”
The workshop focuses on introducing students to innovative, eco-friendly, and sustainable alternatives to conventional concrete and construction materials. It will explore emerging technologies, sustainable material applications, and modern approaches to reducing the environmental impact of the construction industry. The session will bridge theoretical knowledge with practical insights and help students understand the role of sustainable materials in the future of civil engineering.
The workshop is designed for
III and IV Year Civil Engineering students
and features
Dr. Parthiban Kathirvel
, Senior Assistant Professor, School of Civil Engineering, SASTRA Deemed University, Thanjavur, as the Resource Person. The program aims to enhance students’ technical knowledge, encourage sustainable construction practices, and inspire innovative thinking in civil engineering.
