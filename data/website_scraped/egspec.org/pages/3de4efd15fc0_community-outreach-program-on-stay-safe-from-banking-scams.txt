COMMUNITY OUTREACH PROGRAM ON " STAY SAFE FROM BANKING SCAMS
August 12, 2026
10:00 AM
offline Event
The program aimed to create awareness among the community about various types of banking and online financial scams, including fraudulent calls, fake messages, phishing links, OTP scams, and unauthorized banking transactions. The session focused on educating participants about safe banking practices, protecting personal and financial information, and taking appropriate precautions against cyber fraud.
