Workshop on Entrepreneurship Development & Business Opportunities
The Department of Information Technology at EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam, is organizing a workshop on
“Entrepreneurship Development & Business Opportunities”
on 14th October 2026, from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM at the college auditorium.
The workshop will feature
Dr. N. Mathivanan
, Founder & CEO of NM Group of Industries and Research Foundation, as the resource person. The session aims to inspire students to develop an entrepreneurial mindset, explore innovative business ideas, identify emerging business opportunities, and gain insights into starting and managing successful ventures.
