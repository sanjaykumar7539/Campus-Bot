Programme Educational Objectives
Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO
Description
PEO1
Graduates of the Programme will be successful engineers in the field of Computer Science who solve industry problems and social problems.
PEO2
Graduates of the Programme will be engineering professionals, innovators, or entrepreneurs engaged in technology development, deployment, or engineering system implementation in industry.
PEO3
Graduates of the Programme will pursue higher studies in engineering or management and pursue a career path in teaching or research.
