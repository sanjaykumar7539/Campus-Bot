Program Educational Outcomes (PEOs)
#
PEO1
Become a successful Civil Engineer to meet the demand-driven needs in the field of Civil Engineering and related disciplines or pursue higher studies or research or become an entrepreneur.
PEO2
Analyze and design Civil engineering systems with social awareness and responsibilities.
PEO3
Exhibit professionalism, moral values, communication skills, teamwork in their profession, and lifelong learning ability.
