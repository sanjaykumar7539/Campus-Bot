Program Outcomes (POs)
PO #
Program Outcomes Description
PO1
Engineering Knowledge:
Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and specialized cyber defense techniques to the solution of complex computing and cybersecurity problems.
PO2
Problem Analysis:
Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex cybersecurity challenges reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of computer science, cryptography, and network security.
PO3
Design/Development of Solutions:
Design robust security architectures, defense systems, and secure software components that meet specified needs with appropriate consideration for public safety, confidentiality, and data privacy regulations.
PO4
Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems:
Use research-based knowledge and security analysis methods including vulnerability assessments, digital forensics, log forensics, and penetration testing to provide valid security findings.
PO5
Modern Tool Usage:
Create, select, and apply appropriate cybersecurity and forensic tools (such as SIEM, Wireshark, Metasploit, Nessus, Autopsy, and IDS/IPS) to secure modern computing environments with an understanding of limitations.
PO6
The Engineer and Society:
Apply contextual knowledge to assess cyber law, digital compliance, information privacy, and societal responsibilities relevant to the professional cybersecurity practice.
PO7
Environment and Sustainability:
Understand the impact of secure and sustainable digital infrastructure solutions in societal and environmental contexts, promoting sustainable IT operations.
PO8
Ethics:
Apply rigorous ethical principles, adhere to responsible vulnerability disclosure policies, and commit to professional ethics and legal standards in cybersecurity.
PO9
Individual and Team Work:
Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse incident response teams and multidisciplinary security settings.
PO10
Communication:
Communicate effectively on complex security architectures, threat reports, and audit findings with both technical teams and organizational executives.
PO11
Project Management and Finance:
Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of cybersecurity management principles, risk governance, and apply these to manage security projects effectively.
PO12
Life-long Learning:
Recognize the ongoing evolution of cyber threat landscapes and possess the preparation and ability to engage in independent lifelong learning and continuous security certification.
