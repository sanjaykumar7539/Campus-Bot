Program Education Objectives (PEOs)
##
Program Education Objectives
1
Graduates of the Programme, will be successful engineers in the field of Computer Science who solves industry problems and social problems.
2
Graduates of the Programme, will be engineering professionals, innovators or entrepreneurs engaged in technology development, technology deployment, or engineering system implementation in industry.
3
Graduates of the Programme, will be pursuing higher studies in engineering or management and pursue career path in teaching or research.
