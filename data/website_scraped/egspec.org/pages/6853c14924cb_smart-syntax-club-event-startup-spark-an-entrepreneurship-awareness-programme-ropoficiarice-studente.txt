Startup Spark
is an entrepreneurship awareness programme organized by the
Department of Information Technology, EGS Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, under the
Smart Syntax Club
. The event is specially designed for
1st-year IT students
to introduce them to the exciting world of entrepreneurship, innovation, startups, and technology-driven business opportunities.
The programme aims to help students understand how innovative ideas can be transformed into practical solutions and potential businesses. It will provide an opportunity for students to explore the fundamentals of entrepreneurship, identify real-world problems, develop creative solutions, and gain insights into the journey of building a startup.
