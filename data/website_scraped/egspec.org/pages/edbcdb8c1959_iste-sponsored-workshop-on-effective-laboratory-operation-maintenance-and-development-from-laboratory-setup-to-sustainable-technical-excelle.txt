ISTE SPONSORED WORKSHOP ON EFFECTIVE LABORATORY OPERATION, MAINTENANCE AND DEVELOPMENT: FROM LABORATORY SETUP TO SUSTAINABLE TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE
September 19, 2026
10:00 AM
offline Event
The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at
EGS Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam
, is organizing an
ISTE-sponsored technical workshop
focused on strengthening laboratory operations, maintenance practices, and systematic laboratory development.
The workshop is designed particularly for
laboratory technicians from ECE, EEE, Biomedical Engineering (BME), Mechanical Engineering (MECH), and Civil Engineering departments
. It aims to provide participants with practical knowledge and professional insights into managing laboratories efficiently, safely, and sustainably.
