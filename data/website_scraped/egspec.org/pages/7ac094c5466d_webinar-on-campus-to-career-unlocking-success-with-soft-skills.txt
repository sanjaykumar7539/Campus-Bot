Campus to Career: Unlocking Success with Soft Skills
is a webinar organized by the
Department of Computer Science and Engineering, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam
, exclusively for
III-year CSE students
.
The session focuses on the importance of
soft skills in bridging the gap between campus life and professional careers
. Students will gain valuable insights into developing effective communication, interpersonal skills, confidence, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving, and other essential workplace competencies that can support their academic and career growth.
