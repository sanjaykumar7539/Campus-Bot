Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
PSO #
Programme Specific Outcomes Description
PSO1
Apply cybersecurity principles, cryptographic techniques, secure software engineering, network security, and risk management practices to design, implement, evaluate, and manage secure computing systems for protecting digital assets and critical infrastructure.
PSO2
Demonstrate the ability to identify, analyze, prevent, detect, investigate, and respond to cyber threats by utilizing appropriate cybersecurity tools, frameworks, standards, and best practices to build secure and resilient digital systems.
