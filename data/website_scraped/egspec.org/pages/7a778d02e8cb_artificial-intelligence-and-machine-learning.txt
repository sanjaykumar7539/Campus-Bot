Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
About
The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML) at E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College is established to pioneer next-generation cognitive computing, deep learning algorithms, and intelligent autonomous systems. As artificial intelligence transforms every sector of industry and society, our B.Tech. AI & ML programme equips engineers with deep theoretical foundations, algorithmic thinking, and hands-on proficiency in machine learning, deep neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, reinforcement learning, robotics, and generative AI. Supported by cutting-edge GPU-accelerated computing infrastructure and a vibrant research culture, students build scalable, ethical, and transformative AI solutions that address pressing industry challenges and shape the future of intelligent technologies.
Vision
To be an internationally acclaimed center of excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning education, state-of-the-art research, and ethical innovation, nurturing transformative engineers who lead the intelligent technology era.
Mission
To deliver a comprehensive, industry-aligned curriculum combining core computing principles with advanced AI and Machine Learning methodologies.
To promote interdisciplinary research and innovation in deep learning, computer vision, generative AI, and autonomous intelligent systems.
To establish strong industry partnerships and global collaborative networks providing experiential learning and cutting-edge project opportunities.
To cultivate ethical leadership, societal consciousness, and lifelong learning capabilities for sustainable technological advancement.
