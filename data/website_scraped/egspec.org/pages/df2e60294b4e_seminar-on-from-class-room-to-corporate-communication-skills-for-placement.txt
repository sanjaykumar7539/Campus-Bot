SEMINAR ON FROM CLASS ROOM TO CORPORATE : COMMUNICATION SKILLS FOR PLACEMENT
June 05, 2026
01:30 AM
offline Event
The seminar aimed to enhance students' communication abilities and prepare them for future placement opportunities. The resource person emphasized the importance of effective verbal and non-verbal communication, professional etiquette, presentation skills, group discussions, and interview techniques required in the corporate world. Through interactive discussions and practical examples, students gained valuable insights into developing confidence, improving interpersonal skills, and adapting to workplace communication standards. The session motivated participants to start building their communication competencies from the early stages of their academic journey.
