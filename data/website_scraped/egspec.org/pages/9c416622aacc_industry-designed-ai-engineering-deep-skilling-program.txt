Industry-Designed Engineering Program on AI Engineering Deep-Skilling at EGS Pillay Engineering College
EGS Pillay Engineering College
continues to strengthen industry-oriented education with an
Industry-Designed Engineering Program on AI Engineering Deep-Skilling
. The program is designed to help students gain practical knowledge and skills aligned with the evolving needs of the engineering and technology industries.
Bridging Academic Learning with Industry Requirements
As technology continues to transform industries worldwide, engineering graduates need more than theoretical knowledge. They require exposure to real-world technologies, industry practices, problem-solving approaches, and emerging fields such as Artificial Intelligence.
The AI Engineering Deep-Skilling initiative focuses on aligning student learning with real-world engineering requirements and preparing students for technology-driven careers.

Why AI Engineering Deep-Skilling Matters
Artificial Intelligence is increasingly becoming an important part of engineering, manufacturing, software development, automation, healthcare technology, automotive systems, and several other industries. Developing strong AI-related skills can help engineering students understand emerging technologies and become better prepared for future career opportunities.
Through industry-aligned learning, students can strengthen their technical capabilities, practical understanding, analytical thinking, and readiness to work in modern engineering environments.
Academic Excellence and Recognition

NBA Tier I Accreditation for eligible programs
,
NAAC Grade A++
, and other quality and institutional recognitions showcased by the college.
Preparing Future-Ready Engineers
The Industry-Designed Engineering Program on AI Engineering Deep-Skilling reflects EGS Pillay Engineering College's focus on connecting academic education with industry expectations. By providing students with opportunities to develop relevant and practical skills, the institution aims to prepare graduates to confidently meet the challenges of today's rapidly changing technology landscape.
