About the Department
The Department of Computer Applications offers a 2-year full-time MCA (Master of Computer Applications) program designed to equip students with strong theoretical and practical knowledge in computing.
The program was initiated in 2001 with approval from Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirappalli.
In 2003, the course received recognition and approval from Anna University, Chennai.
Since 2011, the department has maintained an annual intake of 60 students.
The institution currently enjoys autonomous status, enabling the department to regularly update the curriculum in alignment with the evolving needs of academia and industry, ensuring students are industry-ready upon graduation.
Vision of the Department
To produce software professionals with Academic Excellence in Computer Applications.
Mission of the Department
Mission Statements
M1 To make software professionals and entrepreneurs by imparting quality teaching and learning practice.
M2 To promote inter-disciplinary research among the faculty and the students to create state of art research facilities.
M3 To prepare the students to be professionally competent to face the challenges in the industry.
M4 To promote quality and ethics among the students.
