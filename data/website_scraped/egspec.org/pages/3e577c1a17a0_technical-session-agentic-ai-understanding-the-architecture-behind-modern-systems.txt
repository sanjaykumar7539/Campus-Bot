TECHNICAL SESSION AGENTIC AI: UNDERSTANDING THE ARCHITECTURE BEHIND MODERN SYSTEMS
September 30, 2026
07:00 AM
online Event
The
Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College (Autonomous), Nagapattinam
, organizes a Technical Session on
“Agentic AI: Understanding the Architecture Behind Modern Systems”
on
30th September 2026, from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM
, through online mode.
The session will be led by
Mr. S. Niranjan, Programmer Analyst, Cognizant Technology Solutions, Chennai
, who will share his industry expertise and insights into the evolving landscape of Agentic Artificial Intelligence.
The programme aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of Agentic AI architectures, intelligent autonomous systems, AI-driven decision-making, and the integration of modern AI technologies into real-world applications. Participants will gain valuable insights into how intelligent agents perceive information, plan actions, execute tasks, and adapt to dynamic environments.
