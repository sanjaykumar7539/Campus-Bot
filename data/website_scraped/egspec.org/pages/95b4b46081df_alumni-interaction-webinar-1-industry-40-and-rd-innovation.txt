Alumni Interaction webinar 1 INDUSTRY 4.0 AND R&D INNOVATION
August 21, 2026
07:00 AM
online Event
The session will be delivered by
Mr. J. Sadhanandham
, Alumni (Batch 2009–2013), Scientist-C at
CSIR – Central Leather Research Institute, Adyar, Chennai
, under the Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India. The interaction will help students understand how modern technologies such as automation, smart manufacturing, digitalization, data-driven systems, and advanced research methodologies are transforming industries.
The webinar will also provide an opportunity for students to interact with an accomplished alumnus, gain practical knowledge from industry and research experience, explore career and higher-education opportunities, and develop an innovative mindset.
