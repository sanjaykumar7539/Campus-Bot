Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
Department Highlights
S.No
Salient Features & Key Highlights
1
GPU-Accelerated AI Research Lab:
High-performance computing clusters powered by NVIDIA GPUs tailored for training deep learning models, computer vision, and transformer networks.
2
Hands-on Framework Training:
Extensive practical coursework covering PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-Learn, OpenCV, Hugging Face, LangChain, and ROS (Robot Operating System).
3
AI Innovation & Machine Learning Club:
Active student community focused on Kaggle competitions, open-source AI contributions, Hackathons, and AI-driven social impact projects.
4
Generative AI & LLM Research Hub:
Dedicated research cells working on Large Language Models, prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and multimodal intelligence.
5
Industry MoUs & Internship Collaborations:
Strategic partnerships with AI product companies, IT conglomerates, and research labs for capstone projects and industrial mentorship.
6
Interdisciplinary Robotics & Autonomous Systems:
Collaborative research across computer vision, smart IoT, autonomous drone navigation, and healthcare diagnostic AI.
7
High-Demand Career Pathways:
Strong placement assistance preparing students for roles as AI Engineers, Machine Learning Scientists, Data Engineers, Computer Vision Specialists, and NLP Engineers.
